# What we do and what it costs

We fix the front door of a local business (its website, Google profile, phone links and quote forms), then put a small assistant behind it that answers, sorts and books while you work. Everything is priced before it starts. Nothing is promised about savings or new revenue; we agree one measurable result and report it.

## Offers

| Offer | What you get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Front-door fix** | Your website restored or rebuilt as a small, fast site; your Google profile claimed and completed; phone numbers that dial when tapped; a quote or booking form that reaches you; directories repointed; renewal and uptime alerts set. Your own registrar or hosting account stays in your hands; we specify and verify, you or your web person click. | Fixed price quoted after a 20-minute walkthrough, usually **$900–1,800**. Optional monitoring **$50–100 a month**. |
| **Intake assistant pilot** | A voice-and-form assistant on your existing number for after-hours and overflow calls: answers, asks your questions, sorts urgent from routine, books or arranges a callback, texts you a summary. Inbound calls only. 2–4 weeks, one agreed success measure, an end date. | **$1,200–1,500**, credited to setup if you continue within 30 days. |
| **Setup and care** | The assistant wired into your booking, case or job system so it writes real appointments and tickets, not messages. Then monthly care: monitoring, tuning, small changes, and a 30-minute review each month. | Setup **$2,500–3,500**. Care **$450–650 a month**, including about 500–2,000 call minutes depending on plan. Three-month minimum. |
| **Back-office assistant** | For firms with a paper-heavy routine: closing-file coordination, quote desk, bid and hearing radar, document chasing, work-order triage. Drafts for your staff to approve; nothing goes out unreviewed. | Pilot **$1,500–2,500**, credited. Setup **$3,500–5,000**. Care **$550–850 a month**. |

## How a pilot works

- Paid, with a named owner on your side, one success measure written down (for example: after-hours calls answered and booked; misdialled call links fixed to zero) and an end date.
- 50% at signing, the rest at the end.
- What is included and excluded is written down; anything extra is quoted in writing before it is done.
- At the end you get the counts and a 30-day window to continue, with the pilot fee credited to setup.

## What we do not do

- Make outbound sales or marketing calls with an AI voice.
- Give legal, medical or financial advice, or quote your fees; the assistant hands those to you.
- Hold your passwords or your registrar or hosting login.
- Promise savings, headcount or revenue. We report what was counted.

## Your data

Your call recordings, messages and customer details are used only to run your assistant, are never used to train anyone's model, and are deleted at the end of a pilot if you ask. A one-page data sheet says where it lives and who can see it.
