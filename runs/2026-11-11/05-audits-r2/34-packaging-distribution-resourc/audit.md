# AI Opportunity Audit — Packaging & Distribution Resources (PDR)

You've grown to 100+ clients, 20 assembly lines and six Edison sites covering 600,000+ sq ft, and since September 2026 a Toronto site runs the same order systems. Yet new business still arrives as a spreadsheet emailed to a general inbox.

## 1. A faster front door for new quotes

**What's happening now:** New business arrives as a downloaded Excel questionnaire emailed to info@ for pricing.

**What the agent would do:** It reads the questionnaire, flags missing answers, sends one follow-up and drafts a quote packet from your rate cards; a person approves every number.

**Expected impact:** 500–800 hours of quote prep a year, roughly $12,000–$25,000, plus same-day response. *(Inference: 15–40 requests monthly, 2–6 hours each.)*

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks on new inquiries, tracking hours-to-quote-ready before and after.

## 2. Fewer orders to fix by hand

**What's happening now:** Orders arrive from 30+ platforms into Microsoft Dynamics, with Order Desk handling multi-channel edits. Toronto runs the same systems with no new corporate roles published, so the same team appears to absorb more.

**What the agent would do:** It watches order events, sorts each problem by type, proposes a fix from that client's rules, then resolves the safe ones or hands a clerk a one-click approve card.

**Expected impact:** 900–2,200 hours of clerk time, roughly $25,000–$60,000 a year; chargebacks unpriced. *(Inference: 1.5–3 staff touch these today.)*

**Pilot:** 10 days read-only on one channel, then three problem types behind a human approval queue.

## 3. One place to ask "where's this order?"

**What's happening now:** 100+ clients across four regions and day and night shifts reach you through one phone number and one generic inbox. Even with the client visibility you advertise, businesses like yours often find answers live in one person's memory of a client's SOP.

**What the agent would do:** It answers from each client's specs, routing guides and live order status, citing the source and staying inside one client; commercial questions go to the owner.

**Expected impact:** 500–1,850 hours of client-service capacity, $12,000–$40,000 a year. The study found 14% more issues per hour, 34% for new staff and no measurable gain for experienced staff; we assume 8–15%. *(Inference; other industry.)*

**Pilot:** Two clients with the richest spec binders, internal-only, scored on answer accuracy.

## Why this works

- **It proposes, you approve.** Nothing prices or ships without someone clicking yes.
- **It fits your stack.** Dynamics and Order Desk stay the record, and client documents stay separate.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough on one live questionnaire, then a small paid pilot on the quote intake.
