# AI Opportunity Audit — Sayrebrook Veterinary Hospital

Your site advertises 24-hour emergency care to the main number, while posted hours are Mon–Thu 8–7, Fri–Sat 8–5 and Sun 8–2. Web-form appointment requests still wait for a staff call-back.

## 1. Someone always answers

**Now:** Web-form requests wait for a call-back and same-day slots are availability dependent.

**What it would do:** Answer calls, texts and the form in English or Spanish, follow a red-flag script your veterinarians approve, page on-call for emergencies and hold slots for staff confirmation.

**Impact:** about 327–728 staff hours a year (3–5 hours per open day, seven days, 30–40% handled) at $28.32/hour (veterinary technician median, NJ, May 2023, ×1.25 benefits) = about $9,260–$20,616 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, after-hours and overflow only.

## 2. One inbox, not six

**Now:** Intake arrives by phone, a Gmail address, a web request form, LifeLearn-hosted forms and printable pages; housecalls and medical boarding have no booking route of their own beyond the general phone, form and email channels.

**What it would do:** Pull those submissions into one queue staff approve instead of retyping, flag incomplete forms, and propose housecall routes and boarding placements.

**Impact:** about 182–509 staff hours a year (2–4 hours per open day, seven days, 25–35% removed) at $28.32/hour (veterinary technician median, NJ, May 2023, ×1.25 benefits) = about $5,154–$14,414 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks on the web form and Gmail; boarding board on day one.

## 3. Answers for many hats

**Now:** Your employment page takes applications year-round and expects new hires to learn "the various protocols and adjust accordingly"; staff profiles show technicians moving from boarding to pharmacy.

**What it would do:** Answer staff questions from your handbook and protocols with the quoted passage — boarding medication schedules and drug-log rules — and a "not covered, ask the lead" fallback.

**Impact:** about 18–96 staff hours a year (2–4 hires × 30–60 hours, 30–40% avoided) at $28.32/hour (veterinary technician median, NJ, May 2023, ×1.25 benefits) = about $509–$2,718 a year; the value is faster ramp-up and cleaner logs.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, boarding and medical boarding only.

## Why this works

- **You keep approval:** no dosing advice, no record written and no billing message sent without staff release.
- **It sits on your tools:** your phone, form, mailbox and hosted forms stay as they are, records sit in an account you own, with role-based access and an audit trail.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough. If it looks useful, a small paid pilot on one opportunity, with figures confirmed in discovery.
