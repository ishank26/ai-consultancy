# AI Opportunity Audit — Benaka, Inc.

You have been building since 1994, largely federal clients by your published list — Air Force, Army and Navy design-build, JOC, IDIQ and maintenance work. Your QC Manager posting reads like a list of daily documents.

## 1. A second pair of hands for daily QC paperwork

**Now:** That posting lists three-phase control, preparatory meetings, minutes, RFIs, purchase orders and daily production reports, and asks for QCS/RMS familiarity alongside Word, Excel and Outlook; a related listing advertises $100,000–$140,000.

**The agent:** Drafts daily reports from field notes and photos, keeps a submittal register with overdue flags, drafts RFIs, minutes and three-phase checklists; your QC manager signs each one.

**Impact:** about 270–450 staff hours a year (6–10 a week over 45 weeks) at $83/hour (construction manager median, NJ, x1.25 for benefits) = about $22,400–$37,300 a year.

**Pilot:** four weeks on one task order, 20 reports and RFIs against your QC manager's own.

## 2. A bid desk that checks each Section L line

**Now:** You bid task orders under an Air Force MACC and a Corps small-business MATOC, and hold JOC/IDIQ work; the MACC's solicitation drew 28 offers.

**The agent:** Watches SAM.gov and your vehicle portals daily, produces a one-page bid/no-bid sheet and drafts the compliance matrix and past-performance sections from your project pages. Estimators keep pricing; you keep go/no-go.

**Impact:** about 22–150 staff hours a year (10–20 pursuits × 15–30 assembly hours, 15–25% rework) at $48/hour (cost estimator median, NJ, x1.25 for benefits) = about $1,000–$7,200 a year.

**Pilot:** one live pursuit, against your last comparable submission.

## 3. Subcontractor and certified-payroll tracking

**Now:** Subs email their details to the general inbox, with no prequalification form; the published intake is phone, fax and email, with nowhere to submit qualifications. NJ public work adds registration certificates and weekly certified payrolls via NJ Wage Hub.

**The agent:** Structured intake, certificate and expiry checks against the state's registered-contractor list, and a weekly payroll pack assembled and flagged before your staff files it. A person approves each filing.

**Impact:** about 180–360 staff hours a year (4–8 a week over 45 weeks) at $36/hour (payroll clerk median, NJ, x1.25 for benefits) = about $6,400–$12,900 a year.

**Pilot:** three weeks, then a month of payrolls alongside today's process.

## Why this works

- **Runs itself:** scheduled mailbox and portal checks.
- **Fits your tools:** Word, Excel, Outlook; QCS/RMS untouched.
- **Human control:** private environment, approval before filing.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot on one task order.
