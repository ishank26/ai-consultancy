# AI Opportunity Audit — Oliver L.E. Soden Agency

Family-owned since 1925, with a Jamesburg headquarters, a Little Silver office and the John B. Wright division in Manasquan, and a 5.0 average across roughly 180 reviews. Your site says the whole Account Management team sits in Jamesburg, so servicing for all three locations likely runs through one team.

## 1. Certificates drafted, you approve

**Now:** Certificate requests route to your CSR24 portal, and the team behind them sits in one office. Businesses like yours often find each certificate typed by hand, then checked.

**An AI assistant would:** read each request from the service inbox or CSR24, pull the policy and draft standard certificates for one-click approval. Unusual wording or limit changes go to a person with a drafted carrier packet. It never binds, cancels or alters coverage.

**Impact:** $20,000–$50,000 a year of account management capacity (0.4–0.8 of a role) — capacity, not a headcount cut.

**Pilot:** 3–4 weeks on marine certificates, timed against your baseline.

## 2. Someone answering after 5pm

**Now:** You advertise 24-hour phone service; published hours are Monday–Friday 9–5, and after-hours cover isn't published. Clients also pick among more than 40 carrier claim and payment portals.

**The assistant would:** on chat and email, identify the client and answer only from a curated list — which portal handles billing, payments and claims, hours, directions — then link them there. Coverage questions go to a licensed staffer; anything else becomes a callback with the transcript.

**Impact:** $12,000–$28,000 a year of service time deflected, plus after-hours enquiries captured.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks: carrier routing map, top 40 questions, draft then live.

## 3. Renewal intake that stops the chasing

**Now:** Quoting starts from one embedded web form, with no upload step visible from outside. Niche pages — contractor, HVAC/electrical/plumbing, pickleball, nonprofit, cyber — bring their own questionnaires.

**The assistant would:** send a secure link for applications, loss runs and schedules, check them against each carrier's requirements, email a plain gap list. Renewals pre-fill at 120/90/60/30 days; an account manager reviews and binds.

**Impact:** $5,000–$16,000 a year of account manager time, plus faster turnaround.

**Pilot:** 4 weeks, one line, one carrier set, 20 historical packets.

## Why this works

- **It asks before it acts.** Nothing binds or changes coverage without a named person's approval.
- **It fits your tools.** Work starts where it already arrives — inbox, CSR24, website form.
- **Client data stays client data.** Access controls, retained transcripts, no training on client files.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough with Hal Soden, Sr. and whoever runs the certificate queue, on sample data; if useful, a small paid pilot, scoped and priced first.
