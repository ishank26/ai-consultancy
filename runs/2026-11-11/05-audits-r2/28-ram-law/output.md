# AI Opportunity Audit — RAM Law

Your site advertises eleven certified civil trial attorneys, $170M+ recovered in ten years, and 24/7 phone intake [home][manalapan]. It also publishes 8am–8pm weekday office hours and closed weekends [manalapan].

## 1. After-hours calls need a first pass

**Now:** Intake is advertised 24/7 [manalapan] and one email, contact@ram.law, serves all three offices, with no portal, scheduling calendar or status lookup [contact].

**The agent:** Answers calls and forms in English or Spanish, records a structured intake, flags deadlines and duplicates, routes by office and books callbacks; injuries go to on-call staff, acceptance stays a lawyer's call.

**Impact:** about 72–480 staff hours a year (150–400 inquiries a month at 6–10 minutes, 40–60% recoverable) at $38/hour (legal-secretary median $30.33 NJ 2025, ×1.25 benefits) = about $2,700–$18,100 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, after-hours and overflow calls only.

## 2. Records and bills chased by hand

**Now:** Your truck-crash list includes organising medical bills, insurer interaction and finding treating specialists [home]; no records or billing role is named among staff [attorney-profile]. Worth checking together: how much of that sits with your paralegals?

**The agent:** Keeps a per-case checklist, drafts records requests and follow-ups, sends each the way providers take them, reads returned PDFs into a ledger and flags gaps.

**Impact:** about 135–1,440 staff hours a year (150–400 matters at 3–6 chase hours each, 30–60% automatable) at $43/hour (paralegal median $34.38 NJ 2025, ×1.25 benefits) = about $5,800–$61,800 a year.

**Pilot:** 20–40 truck or auto matters, one click per letter.

## 3. Status questions in either language

**Now:** Testimonials you publish praise staff who return calls and keep clients updated [home], and the site is fully in Spanish [es]. With no status lookup or live chat [contact], those questions reach staff by phone in office hours (inference).

**The agent:** Answers from your own documents and status fields — handler, records received, next appointment, no-fault explainers — in English and Spanish, citing its source, handing off on treatment, deadlines or money.

**Impact:** about 96–1,200 staff hours a year (100–250 clients, 2–4 status contacts a month at 6–10 minutes, 40–60% deflectable) at $43/hour (paralegal median $34.38 NJ 2025, ×1.25 benefits) = about $4,100–$51,500 a year.

**Pilot:** 25–50 matters, read-only, replies reviewed before sending.

## Why this works

- **Runs itself, then stops.** A written rulebook escalates injuries, deadlines, settlements and low confidence.
- **Fits your tools, keeps people in charge.** Calls, forms and the inbox supply input; acceptance, fees and liens stay human-approved, no training on your data.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough of your after-hours intake, then a small paid pilot on after-hours calls only.
