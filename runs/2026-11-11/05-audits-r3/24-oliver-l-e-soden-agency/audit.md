# AI Opportunity Audit — Oliver L.E. Soden Agency

Independent since 1925, with offices in Jamesburg and Little Silver plus the John B. Wright division in Manasquan, and about 180 Google reviews at 5.0. Your site says the whole Account Management department sits in Jamesburg.

## 1. Certificates drafted, you approve

**Now:** Certificate requests go to your CSR24 portal, and your site puts the whole Account Management department in Jamesburg. Worth checking together: do those requests reach that team?

**What the assistant would do:** read each request from service email or CSR24 and draft a standard certificate for one-click approval; exceptions go to a person. Nothing binds without a named approver.

**Impact:** about 700–1,700 staff hours a year (assumes 2–4 staff on certificate work, 25–45% standard) at $35.95/hour (Insurance Claims and Policy Processing Clerks median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $25,100–$61,100 a year, capacity not headcount.

**Pilot:** 3–4 weeks on marine certificates, against your baseline.

## 2. Someone answering after 5pm

**Now:** You advertise 24-hour phone service, though published hours are Monday–Friday 9–5. Clients also choose among roughly 30 carrier claim and payment portals.

**What the assistant would do:** on chat and email, identify the client, answer from a curated list: which carrier portal handles billing, payments and claims. Coverage questions go to a licensed staffer; the rest becomes a callback.

**Impact:** about 400–800 staff hours a year (assumes 8–15 routine payment/claims questions a day, 30–50% deflectable) at $28.69/hour (Customer Service Representatives median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $11,400–$22,900 a year, plus after-hours enquiries captured.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks: routing map, top 40 questions, then live.

## 3. Renewal intake that stops the chasing

**Now:** Quoting starts from one embedded web form, with no upload step visible from outside. Separate pages cover contractor, HVAC/electrical/plumbing, pickleball, nonprofit and cyber work.

**What the assistant would do:** send a secure link for applications, loss runs and schedules, check them against carrier requirements, email a gap list. Renewals pre-fill at 120/90/60/30 days; a person approves and binds.

**Impact:** about 150–400 staff hours a year (assumes 50–100 shopped submissions and renewals, 2–5 hours saved each) at $49.06/hour (Insurance Sales Agents median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $7,300–$19,600 a year, plus faster turnaround.

**Pilot:** 4 weeks, one line, one carrier set, 20 historical packets.

## Why this works

- **It asks first.** Drafts wait for a named approver; binding stays licensed.
- **It fits your tools.** Starts where requests arrive: email, CSR24, web form.
- **Client data stays client data.** Access controls, retained transcripts, no client-file training.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough with Hal Soden, Sr., on sample data; then, if useful, a small paid pilot.
