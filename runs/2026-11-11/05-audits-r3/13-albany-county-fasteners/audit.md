# AI Opportunity Audit — Albany County Fasteners

You grew from a two-car garage in 2010 to a 25,000 sq ft Edison warehouse, and you quote unlisted and custom parts, C of C/MTR paperwork and Stock & Release programs through your own sales team. Phone, email and chat run Monday–Friday, 8:00–5:00 EST.

## 1. Quotes drafted, not typed

**Happening now:** Your B2B page lists quotes for unlisted parts, C of C/MTR/shop drawings and Stock & Release programs, handled by your in-house sales team.

**The agent would:** read each request from form, email or chat, match part, stock and price tier, and draft the quote and certificates for rep approval.

**Impact:** about 620–1,250 staff hours a year (0.75–1.0 full-time on quotes, 40–60% recovered) at $27.81/hour (order clerk median, NJ, x1.25 for benefits) = about $17,300–$34,700 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks shadow-mode on 100+ real quote lines, measuring minutes per quote.

## 2. After-hours front door

**Happening now:** Public ratings split by platform (4.6 stars over 429 reviews on Birdeye, 2.5 over 21 on Yelp excerpts), with packaging and order resolution the recurring complaints.

**The agent would:** cover chat, the contact form and after-hours voicemail: status answers, address fixes before dispatch, damage claims with photos; refunds go to a person.

**Impact:** about 218–636 staff hours a year (1.5 full-time reps, 50–60% of inquiries automatable, 14–34% gain) at $28.69/hour (customer service rep median, NJ, x1.25 for benefits) = about $6,200–$18,200 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks on chat and email, reporting response times and reopened complaints.

## 3. Keeping two catalogs honest

**Happening now:** You sell through your own store and an Amazon storefront, with piece/pack/bulk price tiers, and target errors below 0.5% of shipped orders per quarter.

**The agent would:** compare the two catalogs on key attributes and price ladders, flag contradictions with a proposed fix, and sample shipped orders against warehouse picks; nothing changes without approval.

**Impact:** about 260–728 staff hours a year (10–20 hours a week on catalog and listing admin, 50–70% recovered) at $28.69/hour (customer service rep median, NJ, x1.25 for benefits) = about $7,400–$20,800 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks read-only on 500–2,000 SKUs, delivering a ranked exception report.

## Why this works

- **Autonomy where it's safe:** status answers go out; money waits for a person.
- **Fits what you run:** plugs into your store, chat, warehouse system and Amazon channel.
- **Your data stays yours:** reads only the product and order details needed, not payment data.

**Suggested first step:** a 20-minute walkthrough of the quote desk with Bob, then a small paid pilot on one workflow, measured in minutes saved.
