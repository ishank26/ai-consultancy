# AI Opportunity Audit — RAM Law

RAM Law's site advertises eleven certified civil trial attorneys, $170M+ recovered in ten years, and bilingual 24/7 intake — a promise built on people rather than systems.

## 1. After-hours calls need a first pass

**Now:** Intake is advertised 24/7, but Manalapan publishes weekday 8am–8pm hours, weekends closed (the others publish none); there is no scheduling or client portal, and one published email, contact@ram.law, serves all three offices unsegmented.

**The agent:** Answers calls and forms in English or Spanish, builds a structured intake record, flags deadlines and duplicates, routes to the right office, books callbacks; injuries reach on-call staff, acceptances stay a lawyer's call.

**Impact:** about 12–30 staff hours a week, roughly $19,000–$47,000 a year at New Jersey support-staff wages, assuming 150–400 inquiries a month of 6–10 minutes.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, after-hours and overflow calls only.

## 2. Records and bills chased by hand

**Now:** The firm's truck-crash list includes medical bills, insurers and treating specialists, and no records or billing role is named on staff — work businesses like yours often absorb into existing paralegals.

**The agent:** Keeps a per-case checklist, drafts records requests and follow-ups, sends each the way providers take them, reads returned PDFs into a ledger (provider, date of service, billed, balance, lien) and flags duplicates and gaps.

**Impact:** roughly 450–1,500 staff hours a year, about $15,000–$50,000 at New Jersey paralegal wages, assuming 150–400 matters at 3–6 chase hours each; plan on $20,000–$40,000.

**Pilot:** 20–40 truck or auto matters, one click on every outbound letter.

## 3. Status questions in either language

**Now:** Testimonials praise staff who return calls and keep clients updated, and the site is fully in Spanish; with no status lookup, every update question is a weekday call — businesses like yours often find that costs per call, not scale.

**The agent:** Answers from the firm's own documents and case status fields — case handler, records received, next appointment, property-damage and no-fault explainers — in English and Spanish, citing its source, with immediate hand-off on treatment, deadlines or money.

**Impact:** about 500–1,100 paralegal hours a year, roughly $17,000–$38,000 at the ~$34/hour New Jersey paralegal median, plus 2–4 attorney hours a week not counted here.

**Pilot:** 25–50 matters, read-only, replies reviewed before sending.

## Why this works

- **Runs itself, then stops.** A written rulebook escalates injuries, deadlines, settlements and low confidence.
- **Fits your tools, keeps people in charge.** Calls, forms and the inbox supply input; acceptance, fees and liens stay human-approved, on a private setup that never trains on your data.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough of your after-hours intake, then a small paid pilot on after-hours calls only.
