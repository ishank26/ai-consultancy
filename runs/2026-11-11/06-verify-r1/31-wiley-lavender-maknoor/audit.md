# AI Opportunity Audit — Wiley Lavender Maknoor

You run a two-partner matrimonial and litigation practice on Amboy Ave, and your site says clients work directly with a partner — no handoffs. It also advertises 200+ cases a year, yet the only ways in are a phone line staffed weekdays 9 to 5 and a form that appears to email the partners.

## 1. Someone answers when you're in court

**Now:** consultations are booked only by phone or form — no calendar, no self-booking — and phone hours are Monday–Friday 9–5 with no after-hours coverage. Inquiries appear to land in the partners' own inboxes.

**The agent:** turns the form into a short guided conversation (matter type, county, key dates), offers real open slots from your calendars, and answers after-hours calls the same way. Conflicts go to a partner.

**Impact:** roughly 130–190 hours a year back, about $3,900–$5,700 at New Jersey legal-secretary pay ($30 an hour), plus 15–40 extra booked consultations.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, 20 inquiries in shadow mode.

## 2. Drafting and deadline support for the volume work

**Now:** your practice list — landlord-tenant, consumer fraud, quiet title, debt collection, Chapter 7, matrimonial — is document-heavy and date-sensitive. No drafting or case-management tooling is visible, and there are no job listings, suggesting no paralegal bench.

**The agent:** fills your own templates, outputs a Word draft with every citation flagged and linked to a real source, and calculates deadlines from a rules table into a shared calendar.

**Caution:** Lexis and Westlaw research tools gave wrong or invented answers 17–33% of the time in a preregistered study, and a general chatbot got roughly 58% of case-specific questions wrong.

**Impact:** roughly 110–190 hours a year ($3,300–$5,700); the real value is avoiding missed deadlines.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, one matter type, 20 drafts you review.

## 3. A private page for each matter

**Now:** no client portal, document upload or payment link — a client who wants to send a pay stub at 8pm has only a message box, while you promise responsiveness.

**The agent:** a secure per-matter link with a document checklist, polite reminders, upload acknowledgements, and status updates drafted from your notes and queued for approval.

**Impact:** roughly 65–130 hours a year ($2,000–$3,900), plus finished document sets sooner.

**Pilot:** 10–15 Chapter 7 or matrimonial matters.

## Why this works

- **You stay in control.** It drafts and routes; a partner approves anything substantive, and conflicts stay human-decided.
- **It fits what you have.** Your current website and calendars, no rip-and-replace; files stay private, access-controlled and logged.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, no preparation needed — then, if useful, a small paid pilot on one area.
