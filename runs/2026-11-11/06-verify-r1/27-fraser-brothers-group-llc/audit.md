# AI Opportunity Audit — Fraser Brothers Group, LLC

Three agency brands — Edison, Belford and Tinton Falls — 17 staff and about 40 carriers. Requests arrive by phone, web form or email, with no online booking, instant quote or chat, and every office closes between 4:15 and 5:00.

## 1. Every request acknowledged within the hour — even Sunday night

**What's happening now:** No booking, instant quote or chat — just a quote form and a phone number. Weekday-only hours differ by office, so Friday-evening requests wait until Monday, and as far as we can tell, someone decides which brand owns it.

**What the agent would do:** Read each form, voicemail and email, ask the three or four questions that matter, tag the line of business, route it to the right office and producer, and draft a reply for approval.

**Expected impact:** Roughly $20,000–$50,000 a year — 0.4–1.0 full-time role at the New Jersey median customer-service wage, loaded.

**Pilot:** Four weeks, Edison office, personal auto and home only.

## 2. Certificates and renewals stop sitting in an inbox

**What's happening now:** Seventeen people cover about 40 carrier markets, including niches like auto dealers, contractors and liquor liability. Client self-service is a generic upload link, so businesses like yours often have someone opening, identifying and routing each file by hand.

**What the agent would do:** Recognise each file, pull the insured, policy numbers, limits and dates, draft the certificate or renewal packet, chase gaps and track deadlines. Uncertain items go to a person.

**Expected impact:** Roughly $25,000–$60,000 a year — one role's worth of intake and chasing at the New Jersey median processing-clerk wage.

**Pilot:** Four weeks, certificate-of-insurance requests only, one office, checked against 100 documents.

## 3. One place to ask "does this cover it?"

**What's happening now:** Staff compare coverage across many forms and about 40 carriers, and you publish most carriers' claims and service numbers as a reference. Mistakes here tend to be the most expensive.

**What the agent would do:** Search your procedures, carrier guides and policy forms, then answer with the exact source, a confidence flag, the carrier contact and a plain-English explanation staff can send the client.

**Expected impact:** Roughly $6,000–$23,000 a year — 15–25% of research time across 0.75–1.5 full-time roles at the New Jersey median customer-service wage.

**Pilot:** Three weeks, top 10–15 carriers, staff only, 150–200 real questions.

## Why this works

- **A licensed person approves everything client-facing** — no prices quoted, no certificate released unreviewed.
- **It fits your existing setup:** quote forms and secure upload portal, plus the calendar and inbox your team already works from, with role-based access and a record of who approved what.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, then one small paid pilot — four weeks, one office — before anything bigger.
