# AI Opportunity Audit — Gaines & Associates, PC

You run an Edison CPA firm that leads with virtual CFO and outsourced accounting for clients nationwide, and still offers tax work. Several lines are sold as recurring monthly services.

### 1. A pre-close assistant for client books
**Now:** Payroll, AP/AR, reconciliations and month-end close are offered as recurring monthly services, and clients send documents through a secure file link.
**Agent:** It matches transactions, queues exceptions with a proposed fix, drafts entries, chases missing documents over ProtectedXchange and assembles the pack; your reviewer approves before posting.
**Impact:** about 225–720 staff hours a year (25–40 books a month x 12 closes x 1.5–3 hours each x 50% handed over, our assumption) at $60.59/hour (accountants and auditors median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits; grade choice our assumption) = about $13,600–$43,600 a year.
**Pilot:** 3–5 client books, 2–4 weeks, timed before and after.

### 2. A first-response assistant that books consults
**Now:** Your consultation page is a form with no calendar or qualifying questions, and a free initial consultation is the offer.
**Agent:** It replies within minutes, asks a short qualifier, books a consult into a partner's calendar and writes a one-page brief.
**Impact:** about 78–156 staff hours a year (52 weeks x 3–6 hours of triage x 50% handed over, our assumption) at $60.59/hour (accountants and auditors median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $4,700–$9,400 a year.
**Pilot:** 2–3 weeks, response times and booked consults logged.

### 3. A private answer desk for tax season
**Now:** Your service pages list tax planning and tax preparation. Worth checking together: how much of that lands in a few months?
**Agent:** It searches your prior returns, workpapers and SOPs, answers with the document cited, declines when nothing supports it, and flags figures that disagree with last year; a preparer signs off.
**Impact:** about 150–500 staff hours a year (3–5 seasonal staff, our assumption, x 500 hours each x 10%–20% of lookup and tie-out time, our assumption) at $26.09/hour (tax preparers median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $3,900–$13,000 a year.
**Pilot:** 3–4 weeks; test it on 10–20 finished returns.

### Why this works
- Your people keep the decisions: the assistant proposes, a reviewer approves before anything posts.
- It sits alongside QuickBooks and your secure file link, with no rip-and-replace.
- The tax desk runs privately in your own environment, client files kept apart.

**Suggested first step:** a 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot on a few books. Worth checking together: how many books close each month?
