# AI Opportunity Audit — Gaines & Associates, PC

You've moved from an Edison tax practice into outsourced accounting and fractional CFO work nationwide while hiring senior tax and accounting leadership — a lot of judgment now sits on a small bench.

### 1. A close assistant for client books
**Now:** You sell recurring reconciliation and month-end close, and the open Senior Accounting Manager role owns multi-client close across QuickBooks, Bill.com and NetSuite.
**Agent:** It matches transactions, queues exceptions with a proposed fix, drafts entries, chases missing documents over ProtectedXchange and assembles the report pack; your reviewer approves.
**Impact:** about 225–720 staff hours a year (300–480 closes = 25–40 books monthly, our assumption, x 1.5–3 hours each x 50% handed over, our assumption) at $60.59/hour (accountants and auditors median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $13,600–$43,600 a year.
**Pilot:** 3–5 client books, 2–4 weeks, timed before and after.

### 2. An after-hours qualifier that books consults
**Now:** The consultation page is a form with no calendar or qualifier, hours are 9–5 weekdays, and a free consultation is advertised.
**Agent:** It replies to form submissions and missed calls within minutes, asks a short qualifier, books a consult into a partner's calendar and writes a one-page brief into HubSpot.
**Impact:** about 78–156 staff hours a year (52 weeks x 3–6 hours of call triage, our assumption, x 50% handed over, our assumption) at $60.59/hour (accountants and auditors median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $4,700–$9,400 a year.
**Pilot:** 2–3 weeks, response times and booked consults logged.

### 3. A private answer desk for tax season
**Now:** Your Tax Director posting calls for building a new tax practice, setting workflow standards and overseeing technology adoption, while tax work compresses into filing season.
**Agent:** It searches your memos, prior returns, workpapers and SOPs, answers with the document cited, declines when nothing supports an answer, flags figures that disagree with last year; a preparer signs off.
**Impact:** about 150–500 staff hours a year (filing season: 3–5 seasonal staff, our assumption, x about 500 seasonal hours each x 10%–20% of lookup and tie-out time, our assumption) at $26.09/hour (tax preparers median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $3,900–$13,000 a year.
**Pilot:** 3–4 weeks; test it on 10–20 finished returns.

### Why this works
- Decisions stay with your people: the assistant proposes, the answer desk flags.
- It fits QuickBooks Online, Bill.com, CCH ProSystem and HubSpot, and the tax desk deploys privately.

**Suggested first step:** a 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot. Worth checking together: how many books close each month?
