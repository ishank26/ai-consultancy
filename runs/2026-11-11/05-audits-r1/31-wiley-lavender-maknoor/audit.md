# AI Opportunity Audit — Wiley Lavender Maknoor

You run a two-partner matrimonial and litigation practice on Amboy Ave, and your site says clients work directly with a partner — no handoffs. It also advertises 200+ cases a year, yet the only ways in are a phone line staffed weekdays 9 to 5 and a web form that lands in your own inbox.

## 1. Someone answers when you're in court

**Now:** consultations are booked only by calling or submitting the form — no calendar, no self-booking. Phone hours are Monday–Friday 9–5 with no after-hours coverage, and inquiries land in the partners' individual inboxes.

**The agent:** turns the form into a short guided conversation (matter type, county, key dates, opposing party), offers real open slots from your calendars, and answers after-hours calls the same way. Conflicts and urgent matters go to a partner to decide.

**Impact:** roughly 130–190 hours a year back (2.5–3.5 a week), about $3,900–$5,800 at New Jersey legal-secretary pay, plus 15–40 extra booked consultations a year. Illustrative.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks; 20 inquiries run in shadow mode first.

## 2. Drafting and deadline support for the volume work

**Now:** your practice list — landlord-tenant, consumer fraud, quiet title, debt collection, Chapter 7, matrimonial — is document-heavy and date-sensitive. No drafting or case-management tooling is visible, and there's no careers page or job listing anywhere we could check, suggesting no paralegal bench.

**The agent:** fills your own templates from a structured matter record, outputs a Word draft with every citation flagged and linked to a real source, and calculates deadlines from a fixed rules table into a shared calendar with reminders. Nothing leaves without your sign-off.

**Caution:** legal AI tools have been measured inventing citations 17–58% of the time, which is why this runs on your templates with mandatory source checks.

**Impact:** roughly 110–190 hours a year ($3,300–$5,800); the bigger value is avoided missed deadlines.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, one matter type, 20 drafts you review.

## 3. A private page for each matter

**Now:** no client portal, document upload or payment link — a client who wants to send a pay stub at 8pm has only a message box, while your stated values are responsiveness and transparency.

**The agent:** a secure per-matter link with a document checklist, polite reminders, upload acknowledgements and status updates drafted from your own notes and queued for your approval.

**Impact:** roughly 65–130 hours a year ($2,000–$3,900), plus faster complete document sets.

**Pilot:** 10–15 Chapter 7 or matrimonial matters.

## Why this works

- **You stay in control.** The agent drafts and routes; a partner approves anything substantive, and conflicts are always human-decided.
- **It fits what you have.** It sits on your current website and calendars — no rip-and-replace.
- **Privacy by design.** Client files stay in a private, access-controlled environment, nothing trains outside models, and every action is logged.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, no preparation needed — then, if useful, a small paid pilot on one area so you see the numbers before committing further.
