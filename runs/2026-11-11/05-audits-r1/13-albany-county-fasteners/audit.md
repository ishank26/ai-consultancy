# AI Opportunity Audit — Albany County Fasteners

You have built something unusual: a family business that started in a two-car garage in 2010 and now ships from a 25,000 sq ft Edison warehouse holding 40M+ pieces, selling piece, pack or bulk with no order minimum to both businesses and homeowners. What stands out is that you do the hard part well — broad stainless and specialty stock, fast shipping, real quotes for unlisted and custom items — while the paperwork around it still runs through a small team.

## 1. A quote desk that drafts the quote before your team touches it

**Happening now:** You advertise quotes for listed *and* unlisted products, custom manufacturing, certificates of conformance and MTRs, blanket release and stock-and-lock programs — each one keyed by hand. Purchasing, fulfillment, marketing and QC each sit with one person, so quote work competes with the warehouse.

**The agent would:** read each request from the quote form, email or chat; match the part, stock and piece/pack/bulk price tier; and draft the quote plus any certificate packet for a rep to approve with one click. Unlisted or custom items are flagged for Peter with the sourcing questions already written.

**Impact (illustrative):** $18,000–$34,000/yr of clerical time reallocated, and same-day instead of next-day quote turnaround.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks running in shadow mode beside your reps, measured in minutes per quote on over 100 real request lines.

## 2. A front door that answers after 5pm — and closes the loop

**Happening now:** Phone, email and chat are staffed Monday–Friday, 8:00–5:00 EST, while you market shipping across the country. Public reviews praise selection and speed but mention packaging and unresolved order problems; ratings also diverge widely by platform (4.6 stars over 429 reviews on one, 2.5 over 21 on another), which suggests older complaints were never answered.

**The agent would:** cover chat, the contact form and after-hours voicemail; answer "where is my order", fix an address or quantity before dispatch, and open a damage claim with photos. Refunds, credits and terms changes go to a person. It re-checks 72 hours later that the promised fix actually happened.

**Impact (illustrative):** $10,000–$25,000/yr of support time, plus inquiries captured that currently wait overnight.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks on chat and email, reporting first-response time and reopened complaints before and after.

## 3. Keeping two catalogs honest across 40M+ pieces

**Happening now:** You sell on your own store and an Amazon storefront, stock many grades, materials and finishes with tiered pricing, set yourselves a target of under 0.5% order errors — and you are mid-expansion with new racking and bagging equipment. That combination is where part numbers, pack quantities and prices drift.

**The agent would:** compare your store and marketplace listings on key attributes and price ladders, flag contradictions with a proposed correction, and sample shipped orders against warehouse picks daily. Nothing changes in the live catalog without approval.

**Impact (illustrative):** $8,000–$20,000/yr of catalog admin time, plus fewer wrong-part shipments.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks read-only on 500–2,000 SKUs in one product family, delivering a ranked exception report.

## Why this works

- **Autonomy where it's safe:** routine lookups and status answers go out on their own; anything with money or a binding price waits for a person.
- **Fits what you already run:** it works with your existing store platform, chat widget, warehouse system and Amazon channel rather than replacing them.
- **Your data stays yours:** the agent reads only the product and order details it needs, with payment information kept out of reach.

**Suggested first step:** a 20-minute walkthrough of the quote desk idea with Bob, then a small paid pilot on one workflow so you see measured minutes saved before committing to anything larger.
