# AI Opportunity Audit — Albany County Fasteners

You grew from a two-car garage in 2010 to a 25,000 sq ft Edison warehouse holding 40M+ pieces, sold piece, pack or bulk with no minimum. The hard part is done well — deep stainless stock, fast shipping, real quotes for unlisted items — but the paperwork still runs through a small team.

## 1. A quote desk that drafts the quote first

**Happening now:** You quote listed and unlisted products, custom parts, certificates and MTRs; businesses like yours often prepare these by hand through the sales team, where purchasing, fulfillment and marketing each sit with one person.

**The agent would:** read each request from the form, email or chat; match part, stock and price tier; and draft the quote and certificate packet for rep approval. Custom items are flagged for Peter.

**Impact (illustrative):** $18,000–$34,000/yr of clerical time, plus faster turnaround measured in the pilot.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks in shadow mode, tracking minutes per quote on 100+ real lines.

## 2. A front door that answers after 5pm

**Happening now:** Phone, email and chat are staffed Monday–Friday, 8:00–5:00 EST while you ship nationwide. Reviews praise selection and speed but mention packaging and unresolved problems, and ratings diverge by platform; at businesses like yours that gap often means older complaints went unanswered.

**The agent would:** cover chat, the contact form and after-hours voicemail; answer status questions, fix an address before dispatch, and open a damage claim with photos. Refunds go to a person.

**Impact (illustrative):** $6,000–$18,000/yr of support time, plus after-hours inquiries captured.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks on chat and email, reporting response times and reopened complaints.

## 3. Keeping two catalogs honest

**Happening now:** You sell on your own store and an Amazon storefront, stock many grades with tiered pricing, and hold yourselves to an order error target under 0.5%.

**The agent would:** compare the two catalogs on key attributes and price ladders, flag contradictions with a proposed correction, and sample shipped orders against warehouse picks. Nothing changes without approval.

**Impact (illustrative):** $8,000–$20,000/yr of catalog admin time, plus fewer wrong-part shipments.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks read-only on 500–2,000 SKUs, delivering a ranked exception report.

## Why this works

- **Autonomy where it's safe:** status answers go out; anything with money waits for a person.
- **Fits what you run:** your store, chat, warehouse system and Amazon channel, not replacements.
- **Your data stays yours:** it reads only the product and order details it needs, never payment data.

**Suggested first step:** a 20-minute walkthrough of the quote desk with Bob, then a small paid pilot on one workflow to show measured minutes saved.
