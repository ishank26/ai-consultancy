# AI Opportunity Audit — RCP Management Company

Your 2023 merger announcement reported 125 associations and 15,000 doors; the firm runs three offices. A Princeton onsite administrator posting lists answering calls, entering work orders and back-charging owner accounts.

## 1. A front desk with a callback queue
**Now:** Contact is a phone-and-email directory plus one web form, with weekday 9–5 hours per your Yelp/Yahoo Local listing. Staff review new portal access requests and promise a reply within two business days. A recent review describes repeated calls that were not returned.
**The agent would:** Read email, web-form and transcribed calls, identify the community and unit, draft a reply, open a callback task. Money, fines and legal questions wait for a manager.
**Impact:** about 78–312 staff hours a year per piloted community (10–20 hours a week, 15–30% saved) at $32.50/hour ($26/hour Princeton administrator rate, ×1.25 for benefits) = about $2,535–$10,140 a year per community. A customer-support study found 14% average gains, 34% for newer staff.
**Pilot:** 3–5 communities, 2–4 weeks, one response-time report.

## 2. Work orders that enter themselves
**Now:** An RCP job-board posting lists the duties: preparing work orders from resident and manager requests, entering them, back-charging owners and updating Buildinglink.
**The agent would:** Pull unit, location, problem and urgency, suggest trade and vendor, draft the work order and back-charge with the rule cited. A manager approves each back-charge.
**Impact:** about 333–1,333 staff hours a year (5,000–10,000 tickets at 4–8 minutes each) at $30.20/hour (secretary/administrative-assistant median, NJ, ×1.25 for benefits) = about $10,056–$40,256 a year.
**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks against a control group.

## 3. One place to ask about a community
**Now:** Two firms merged in 2023 into three offices. Worth checking together: two sets of procedures, two document libraries?
**The agent would:** Answer staff and board questions on bylaws, collections, architectural requests and vendor terms, citing clause and page, each community's documents kept separate. Unanswered questions become a monthly fix-list.
**Impact:** about 83–624 staff hours a year (20–30 managers and assistants, 1–2 hours a week each, 20–50% less lookup time, 40% converted) at $49.79/hour (NJ property/community manager median, ×1.25 for benefits) = about $4,132–$31,068 a year.
**Pilot:** 3–4 weeks, 3–5 communities, checked against manager-written answers.

## Why this works
- **It runs itself, then stops.** Routine requests move; money and legal matters wait for approval.
- **It fits your tools.** Work lands in your portal and Buildinglink records, not a new system.
- **Data stays separate.** Each association's documents are kept apart, with a log.

## Suggested first step
A 20-minute walkthrough on one live community, then a small paid pilot.
