# AI Opportunity Audit — Rich's Plumbing Heating & Air Conditioning, Inc.

Serving New Jersey since 1989: three offices, a 4.7 Google rating for East Brunswick and an advertised 60-minute emergency response. Contact paths lead to one number or a two-step form, with no slot picker online.

## 1. Answering the phone after hours

**Now:** Three offices share one published number; the emergency page lists the safety questions staff ask. Worth checking together: how nights are covered today?

**Agent:** Answers overflow and after-hours calls, asks the safety questions, sorts emergencies from routine work, then books or takes a callback. No price quotes; hazards go to the on-call technician.

**Impact:** about 728–1,664 staff hours a year (0.35–0.8 of a full-time seat, ASSUMPTIONS) at $28.69–$32.81/hour (BLS OEWS Customer Service Representative and Dispatcher medians, NJ, May 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $20,886–$54,590 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, after-hours and overflow only.

## 2. Following up on quiet estimates

**Now:** As far as the site shows, estimates are handled in person during the visit, with nothing visible after. Reviews call pricing fair but "a little high."

**Agent:** When an estimate goes quiet, drafts a recap, an answer to the objection, a day-10 reminder and a day-21 close using your coupons and financing. Staff approve before sending.

**Impact:** about 2–6 recovered jobs a month (ASSUMPTIONS: 40–120 estimates a month, 2–5% recovery, no staff hours claimed) at $400–$1,200 a job and 30–40% margin = about $2,880–$34,560 a year in gross profit.

**Pilot:** 3 weeks against the last 60 days of open estimates.

## 3. Keeping plan records findable

**Now:** Your plan promises seasonal check-ups, priority scheduling and records of equipment and service history. We can't tell from outside where those records live — worth checking together?

**Agent:** Brings them into one searchable place, then answers plain questions: a serial and warranty date, last spring's quote, who's due a tune-up. Answers cite the record; messages are drafted for approval.

**Impact:** about 156–312 staff hours a year (3–6 hours a week of lookups and reminder lists, ASSUMPTIONS) at $32.81/hour (BLS OEWS Dispatcher median, NJ, May 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $5,117–$10,235 a year.

**Pilot:** 3 weeks; 30 records checked field by field.

## Why this works

- **Bounded.** Quotes, diagnoses and warranty calls stay with your people.
- **Fits your tools.** Your form, SMS consent, financing link and coupons exist.
- **Your data stays yours.** Records sit in your systems, not a consumer chatbot.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot on evening calls.
