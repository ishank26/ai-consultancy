# AI Opportunity Audit — Packaging & Distribution Resources (PDR)

600,000+ sq ft across six Edison sites, 20 assembly lines and 100+ clients, plus a Toronto site live since September 2026 on the same order systems. Your published route to a price is still a downloaded Excel questionnaire emailed to info@.

## 1. A faster front door for new quotes

**What's happening now:** The pricing page asks prospects to download the New Customer Questionnaire and email it to info@; the contact page offers one phone number and a form, with no calculator or client login.

**What the agent would do:** It reads each questionnaire, flags missing answers, sends one follow-up and drafts a quote packet from your rate cards; a person approves the numbers before anything goes out.

**Expected impact:** about 480–720 staff hours a year (20–30 requests a month at 4 hours each, half saved) at $28.68/hour (customer service rep median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $13,700–$20,600 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks on new inquiries, tracking hours-to-quote-ready before and after.

## 2. Fewer order exceptions handled by hand

**What's happening now:** Orders arrive from 30+ platforms into Microsoft Dynamics, with Order Desk handling multi-channel edits and fulfilment rules; Toronto runs the same systems.

**What the agent would do:** It watches order events, sorts problems by type, proposes a fix from that client's rules, resolves the safe reversible ones and queues the rest for one-click approval.

**Expected impact:** about 930–2,180 staff hours a year (1.5–3.0 staff on exceptions today, 30–35% of that handled by the agent) at $28.68/hour (customer service rep median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $26,600–$62,500 a year.

Worth checking together: how many people touch one exception today?

**Pilot:** 10 days read-only on one channel, then three exception types behind an approval queue.

## 3. One place to ask "where's this order?"

**What's happening now:** 100+ clients in four regions reach you through one phone number and a general inbox; your technology page describes per-client setup in Dynamics.

**What the agent would do:** It answers from each client's specs, routing guides and live order status, citing the source and staying inside that client; commercial questions go to the account owner.

**Expected impact:** about 490–1,870 staff hours a year (3–6 staff in client service, an 8–15% lift) at $28.68/hour (customer service rep median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $14,000–$53,600 a year.

**Pilot:** Two clients, internal-only, scored on answer accuracy.

## Why this works

- **It proposes, you approve.** Nothing is priced or shipped without a person clicking yes.
- **It fits your stack.** Dynamics and Order Desk stay the record.
- **Client data stays separate.** Each client's documents sit in their own space.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough on one live questionnaire, then a small paid pilot on quote intake.
