# AI Opportunity Audit — Packaging & Distribution Resources (PDR)

You run six Edison sites, 600,000+ sq ft and 100+ clients, with Toronto on the same order systems since September 2026. Your published route to a price is a downloaded Excel questionnaire emailed to info@pdrnj.com.

## 1. A faster front door for quotes

**What's happening now:** Your pricing page asks prospects to download the New Customer Questionnaire and email it to your pricing inbox; there is no calculator or client login.

**What the agent would do:** It reads incoming questionnaires, flags missing answers, sends one follow-up and drafts a quote packet from your rate cards for approval.

**Expected impact:** about 126–1,584 staff hours a year (15–40 requests a month × 12 × 2–6 hours each × 35–55% saved; our assumptions) at $28.69/hour (customer service rep median, NJ, 2025, ×1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $3,610–$45,440 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks on new inquiries, tracking hours-to-quote-ready before and after.

## 2. Fewer order exceptions by hand

**What's happening now:** You integrate 30+ front-end platforms with Microsoft Dynamics, with Order Desk for multi-channel order edits and fulfillment automation; Toronto runs the same systems.

**What the agent would do:** It watches order events, sorts problems by type, proposes a fix from that client's rules, resolves reversible ones and queues the rest for approval.

**Expected impact:** about 900–3,000 staff hours a year (our assumption: 1.5–3.0 people from your published roles × 2,000 hours a year × 30–50% handled by the agent) at the same $28.69/hour loaded rate = about $25,820–$86,070 a year.

Worth checking together: how many people touch one exception today?

**Pilot:** 10 days read-only on one channel, then three exception types behind approvals.

## 3. One place to ask "where's this order?"

**What's happening now:** 100+ clients across four regions, one contact page: a single phone number and a general form; the technology page says Microsoft Dynamics is used to manage each client's business.

**What the agent would do:** It answers from that client's specs, routing guides and live order status, with citations; commercial questions go to the account owner.

**Expected impact:** about 480–1,800 staff hours a year (our assumption: 3–6 people from your published roles × 2,000 hours a year × 8–15% saved) at the same $28.69/hour loaded rate = about $13,770–$51,640 a year.

**Pilot:** Two clients, internal-only, scored on answer accuracy.

## Why this works

- **It proposes, you approve.** Nothing is priced or shipped without a click.
- **It fits your stack.** Dynamics and Order Desk stay the record.
- **Client data stays separate.** Documents sit in that client's own space.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough on one live questionnaire, then a small paid pilot.
