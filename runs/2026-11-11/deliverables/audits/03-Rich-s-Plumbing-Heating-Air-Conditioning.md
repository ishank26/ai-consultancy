# AI Opportunity Audit — Rich's Plumbing Heating & Air Conditioning, Inc.

Family-owned since 1989, three offices, and 24/7 emergency service advertised with a 60-minute response. Booking runs through one number or a two-step form: a request, not a pickable time slot.

## 1. The phone after hours

**Now:** Three offices share one number; the emergency page lists the safety questions staff ask. Worth checking: how nights are covered, and whether "online scheduling" is the form or a calendar behind it?

**Agent:** Answers overflow and after-hours calls, asks those safety questions, sorts emergencies from routine work, then books a visit or a callback. No price quotes; hazards go to the on-call technician.

**Impact:** about 832–1,456 staff hours a year (2,080 hours a seat x 2.0–3.5 seats x 20% handled, our assumptions) at $32.81/hour (NJ dispatcher median, BLS 2025, x1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $27,297–$47,771 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, limited to after-hours and overflow calls.

## 2. Following up on quiet estimates

**Now:** The site shows estimates discussed in person, with nothing visible after; reviews mention pricing — one calls it "a little high."

**Agent:** When an estimate goes quiet, drafts a recap, an answer to the objection, and day-10 and day-21 reminders using your coupons and financing. Staff approve before sending.

**Impact:** 40–120 estimates a month x 2–5% recovered = 0.8–6 jobs a month (9.6–72 a year) x $400–$1,200 a job x 30–40% margin = about $1,150–$34,560 a year in gross profit; the low end is below pilot cost.

**Pilot:** 3 weeks on the last 60 days of open estimates.

## 3. Keeping plan records findable

**Now:** The plan promises seasonal check-ups, priority scheduling and kept equipment and service records; where those records live is not visible. Worth checking together?

**Agent:** Puts them in one searchable place, then answers plain questions (a serial, warranty date, last quote, who is due a tune-up) citing the record behind each answer; messages wait for staff approval.

**Impact:** 52 weeks x 3–6 hours a week of lookups and reminder lists (our assumption) = 156–312 staff hours a year at $32.81/hour (NJ dispatcher median, BLS 2025, x1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $5,118–$10,236 a year.

**Pilot:** 3 weeks; 30 records checked field by field.

## Why this works

- **Bounded.** Quotes, diagnoses and warranty calls stay with your people.
- **Fits your tools.** Your form, SMS consent, financing and coupons exist.
- **Your data stays yours.** Records sit in your systems, not a consumer chatbot.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot on evening calls.
