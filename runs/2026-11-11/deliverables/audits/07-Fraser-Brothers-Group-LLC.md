# AI Opportunity Audit — Fraser Brothers Group, LLC

Three agency brands in Edison, Belford and Tinton Falls, with 17 staff and about 40 carriers. Requests arrive by phone, web form or email; the offices close between 4:15 and 5:00.

## 1. Requests answered within the hour

**What's happening now:** The site offers quote and contact forms and a phone number, with no online booking, instant quote or chat. Worth checking together: what happens to a request sent at 6pm?

**What the agent would do:** Read each form, voicemail and email, ask the questions that matter, tag the business line and draft a routed reply for approval.

**Expected impact:** about 832–2,080 staff hours a year (0.4–1.0 role of intake) at $28.69/hour (NJ customer service rep median, 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $23,868–$59,670 a year.

**Pilot:** Four weeks, Edison office, personal lines.

## 2. Certificates and renewals out of the inbox

**What's happening now:** Seventeen people cover about 40 carrier markets; self-service is a generic secure-upload link. Worth checking together: who opens and routes each file?

**What the agent would do:** Recognise each file, pull the insured, limits and dates, draft the certificate or renewal packet, chase gaps and track deadlines; uncertain items go to a person.

**Expected impact:** about 832–2,080 staff hours a year (0.4–1.0 role of document intake) at $35.95/hour (NJ claims and policy processing clerk median, 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $29,910–$74,776 a year.

**Pilot:** Four weeks, certificate requests only, 100 documents.

## 3. One place for cover questions

**What's happening now:** You publish each carrier's claims numbers; the claims page sends clients to the office in business hours. Worth checking together: how much of the day goes on carrier lookups?

**What the agent would do:** Search your procedures, carrier guides and policy forms, then answer with the exact source and the carrier contact, ready for a licensed person to send.

**Expected impact:** about 234–780 staff hours a year (15–25% of research time across 0.75–1.5 roles) at $28.69/hour (NJ customer service rep median, 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $6,712–$22,376 a year.

**Pilot:** Three weeks, top 10–15 carriers, staff only, 150–200 questions.

## Why this works

- **A licensed person approves anything client-facing:** no prices quoted, no certificate released.
- **It plugs into what you run:** your quote form, the secure upload portal and whatever calendar and inbox you use.
- **Client files stay in your systems,** with role-based access, and unsure answers go to a person.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot.
