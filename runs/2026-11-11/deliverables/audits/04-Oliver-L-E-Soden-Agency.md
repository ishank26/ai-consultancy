## 1. Certificates drafted, you approve

**Now:** Certificate requests go through CSR24, and your Jamesburg page says Account Management sits there. Worth checking together: how many arrive weekly, and who drafts them?

**The assistant:** reads each request from the service inbox or CSR24 and drafts the standard certificate for one-click approval; exceptions reach a person.

**Impact:** about 315–1,134 staff hours a year (2–4 staff, our assumption, x 1,800 hours x 35% of time on certificates, our assumption, x 25–45% template-standard) at $35.95/hour (Insurance Claims and Policy Processing Clerks median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $11,300–$40,700 a year.

**Pilot:** 3–4 weeks on marine certificates, against your baseline.

## 2. Consistent answers after 5pm

**Now:** You advertise 24-hour phone service against Monday–Friday 9–5 hours, and your claims page lists 44 carriers with their own claim and payment links. Worth checking together: what do after-hours callers reach?

**The assistant:** identifies the client and points them to the right carrier link for billing, payments or claims; coverage questions go to a licensed staffer.

**Impact:** about 100–468 staff hours a year (our assumption: 8–15 questions a business day x 250 days = 2,000–3,750, x 10–15 minutes, x 30–50% self-service-answerable) at $28.68/hour (Customer Service Representatives median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $2,800–$13,400 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks: routing map, top 40 questions, then live.

## 3. Renewal intake that stops the chasing

**Now:** Quoting starts from one embedded form with no upload step visible from outside; separate pages cover contractor, HVAC/electrical/plumbing, pickleball, nonprofit and cyber work.

**The assistant:** sends a secure link for applications, loss runs and schedules, checks them against carrier requirements and emails a gap list; renewals pre-fill at 120/90/60/30 days, and a licensed staffer reviews and binds.

**Impact:** about 100–500 staff hours a year (50–100 shopped submissions and renewals, our assumption, x 2–5 hours of chasing and re-keying saved each) at $49.06/hour (Insurance Sales Agents median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $4,900–$24,500 a year.

**Pilot:** 4 weeks, one line, one carrier set, 20 historical packets.

## Why this works

- **A person approves.** Drafts wait for a named approver.
- **It fits your tools:** email, CSR24, web form.
- **Data stays yours:** access controls, retained transcripts, no training on client files.
