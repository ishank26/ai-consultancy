What we do and what it costs

We fix the front door of a local business (its website, Google profile, phone links and quote forms), then put a small assistant behind it that answers, sorts and books while you work. Everything is priced before it starts. Nothing is promised about savings or new revenue; we agree one measurable result and report it.

How a pilot works

  • Paid, with a named owner on your side, one success measure written down (for example: after-hours calls answered and booked; misdialled call links fixed to zero) and an end date.
  • 50% at signing, the rest at the end.
  • What is included and excluded is written down; anything extra is quoted in writing before it is done.
  • At the end you get the counts and a 30-day window to continue, with the pilot fee credited to setup.

What we do not do

  • Make outbound sales or marketing calls with an AI voice.
  • Give legal, medical or financial advice, or quote your fees; the assistant hands those to you.
  • Hold your passwords or your registrar or hosting login.
  • Promise savings, headcount or revenue. We report what was counted.

Your data

Your call recordings, messages and customer details are used only to run your assistant, are never used to train anyone's model, and are deleted at the end of a pilot if you ask. A one-page data sheet says where it lives and who can see it.
