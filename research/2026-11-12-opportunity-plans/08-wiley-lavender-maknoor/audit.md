# AI Opportunity Audit — Wiley Lavender Maknoor

You are a two-lawyer matrimonial and commercial-litigation practice on Amboy Ave, with real estate, business and estate work too; your site reports 200+ cases a year and promises clients direct partner access.

## 1. Someone answers when you're in court

**Now:** bookings run through the phone line (Mon–Fri 9–5) or the web form, with no calendar or self-booking visible and no after-hours line mentioned; a partner email is also published.

**The agent:** turns the form into a short guided conversation (matter type, county, key dates), offers real open slots from your calendars, and answers after-hours calls the same way. Conflicts route to a partner.

**Impact:** about 66–245 staff hours a year (400–700 inquiries, 25–35 minutes each, 40–60% removed) at $37.91/hour (legal-secretary median, NJ, 2025, ×1.25 for benefits) = about $2,500–$9,287 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, 20 inquiries in shadow mode.

## 2. Drafting and deadline support

**Now:** your practice list — landlord-tenant, consumer fraud, quiet title, debt collection, Chapter 7 — is document-heavy and date-sensitive, and no drafting or case-management tooling is visible.

**The agent:** fills your templates, returns a Word draft with each citation linked to its source, and calculates deadlines into a shared calendar.

**Caution:** research tools from Lexis and Westlaw gave wrong or invented answers 17–33% of the time in a preregistered study.

**Worth checking together:** who drafts and calendars today?

**Impact:** about 75–200 staff hours a year (200 matters, 1.5–2.5 hours each, 25–40% removed) at $37.91/hour (legal-secretary median, NJ, 2025, ×1.25 for benefits) = about $2,843–$7,582 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, one matter type, 20 drafts.

## 3. A private page for each matter

**Now:** no client portal, document upload or payment link is advertised; a client sending a pay stub at 8pm has a message box or a partner's published email.

**The agent:** a secure per-matter link with a document checklist, reminders, upload acknowledgements, and status updates drafted from your notes for partner approval.

**Impact:** about 62–130 staff hours a year (3–5 hours a week of chasing, 40–50% removed) at $37.91/hour (legal-secretary median, NJ, 2025, ×1.25 for benefits) = about $2,350–$4,928 a year.

**Pilot:** 10–15 Chapter 7 or matrimonial matters.

## Why this works

- **You stay in control.** It drafts, routes and reminds; a partner approves anything substantive, and conflicts stay human-decided.
- **It fits what you have.** Your website and calendars, with files private, access-controlled and logged.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, no preparation needed — then, if useful, a small paid pilot on one area.
