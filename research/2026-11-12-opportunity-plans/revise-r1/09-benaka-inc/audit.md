# AI Opportunity Audit — Benaka, Inc.

You have been building since 1994, with a client list running from the Air Force, Army and Navy to the Smithsonian and municipal agencies. Your QC Manager posting lists the daily documents the role owns: production reports, control forms, minutes, RFIs.

## 1. A second pair of hands for QC paperwork

**Now:** The posting lists three-phase control, preparatory meetings, minutes, RFIs, purchase orders and daily production reports, requires Word, Excel and Outlook and lists QCS/RMS as a plus; a related listing advertises $100,000–$140,000.

**The agent:** Drafts daily reports from field notes and photos, keeps a submittal register with overdue flags, drafts RFIs and minutes; your QC manager signs each.

**Impact:** about 135–315 staff hours a year (45 weeks × 6–10 hours × 50–70% drafted, our assumption) at $72/hour (midpoint of your posted $100,000–$140,000 band, $58/hour × 1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $9,700–$22,600 a year.

**Pilot:** four weeks on one task order, measured against your QC manager's.

## 2. A bid desk that checks each Section L line

**Now:** You hold an Air Force MACC IDIQ and are now in the pool for a Corps small-business MATOC; the MACC solicitation drew 28 offers.

**The agent:** Watches SAM.gov and your vehicle portals, produces a one-page bid/no-bid sheet, drafts the compliance matrix and past-performance text from your project pages; estimators keep pricing.

**Impact:** about 75–420 staff hours a year (10–20 pursuits × 15–30 hours × 50–70% drafted, our assumption) at $48/hour (cost estimator median, NJ, ×1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $3,600–$20,100 a year.

**Pilot:** one live pursuit, against your last comparable submission.

## 3. Subcontractor and certified-payroll tracking

**Now:** Subs email details to the inbox; no prequalification form or vendor portal is published, and vendors are told to phone, email or use the contact page. NJ public work adds certificates and weekly certified payrolls through NJ Wage Hub.

**The agent:** Structured intake, certificate and expiry checks against state records, and a weekly payroll pack flagged before your staff files it; a person approves each filing.

**Impact:** about 90–252 staff hours a year (45 weeks × 4–8 hours × 50–70% prepared, our assumption) at $36/hour (payroll clerk median, NJ, ×1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $3,200–$9,000 a year.

**Pilot:** three weeks, then a month of payrolls alongside today's process.

Worth checking: how much of the book is NJ public work?

## Why this works

- **Runs itself:** scheduled mailbox and portal checks.
- **Fits your tools:** Word, Excel, Outlook; QCS/RMS untouched.
- **Human control:** an environment you control, approval before anything is filed.

**Suggested first step:** a 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot on one task order.
