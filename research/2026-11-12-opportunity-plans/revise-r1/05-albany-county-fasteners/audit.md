# AI Opportunity Audit — Albany County Fasteners

You grew from a two-car garage in 2010 to a 25,000 sq ft Edison warehouse, quoting unlisted parts and C of C/MTR packets through your sales team, weekdays 8:00–5:00 EST.

## 1. Quotes drafted, not typed

**Happening now:** Your B2B page lists quotes on unlisted parts, C of C/MTR packets and Stock & Release programs.

**The agent would:** read each request from form, email or chat, match part, stock and tier price, and draft quote and certificates for approval.

**Impact:** about 500–1,500 staff hours a year (50–100 requests a week x 50 weeks x 30 minutes x 40–60% pre-built — volume, time and share are our assumption) at $27.81/hour (order clerk median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $13,900–$41,700 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks shadow-mode on 100+ real quote lines.

## 2. After-hours front door

**Happening now:** Ratings split: 4.6 stars over 429 reviews on Birdeye, 2.5 over 21 on Yelp excerpts, where complaint reviews cite packaging and one an unresolved order.

**The agent would:** cover chat, contact form and after-hours voicemail: status answers, address fixes before dispatch, photo damage claims; refunds go to a person.

**Impact:** about 300–750 staff hours a year (40–60 inquiries a day x 250 business days x 12 minutes x 15–25% absorbed — volume, time and share are our assumption, kept below the 34% novice figure from a large software vendor's support desk) at $28.69/hour (customer service rep median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $8,600–$21,500 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks on chat and email, tracking response times and reopened complaints.

## 3. Two catalogs, one truth

**Happening now:** You sell through your own store and an Amazon storefront, with piece/pack/bulk tiers, and target order errors below 0.5% of shipped orders per quarter.

**The agent would:** compare the two catalogs on attributes and price ladders, flag contradictions with proposed fixes, and sample shipped orders against picks; catalog writes wait for approval.

**Impact:** about 250–700 staff hours a year (10–20 hours a week x 50 weeks x 50–70% — weekly hours are our assumption) at $28.69/hour (customer service rep median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $7,100–$20,000 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks read-only on 500–2,000 SKUs, with a ranked exception report.

## Why this works

- **Low-risk answers go out; money waits for a person.**
- **Fits your store, chat, warehouse system and Amazon channel.**
- **Reads product and order details, not payment data.**

**Suggested first step:** a 20-minute walkthrough of the quote desk with Bob, then a small paid pilot on one workflow.

Worth checking together: quote requests per week, and minutes per quote?
