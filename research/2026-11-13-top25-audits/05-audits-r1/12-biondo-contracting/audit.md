## 1. Your front door leads to spam
**Now:** Desktop and phone loads show the slot-game page; the Google, ProvenExpert and Webflow pages still link to it.
**We'd:** clean the install, publish a 5–7 page site with a photo-upload quote form, one phone/address/hours set, repointed listings and a change alert.
**Impact:** quote requests arrive as job sheets instead of dead links; the admin saving is smaller — about 3–7 staff hours a year (12–24 inquiries x 15–20 minutes each x 100% captured) at $28.69/hour (BLS NJ customer-service-representative median, x1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $86–$200 a year.
**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, ending with the site live and the link fixed.

## 2. Calls that ring out

**Worth checking together:** how many calls ring out after hours or during tear-offs?

**Impact:** about 117–364 staff hours a year (780–1,560 inquiries x 15–20 minutes each x 60–70% handled, our assumptions) at $28.69/hour (same BLS rate) = about $3,356–$10,442 a year.
**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, with call capture measured.

## 3. A record for every roof
**Now:** Reviews describe leak re-checks, a next-day shingle repair, free plywood replacement and one complaint about calls after payment; no job-record tool was visible.
**Worth checking together:** where do job photos and warranty registrations live?
**We'd:** property-keyed job records, a phone photo checklist, an auto-built roof passport with warranty dates and manufacturer registration, and callbacks drafted for Sam's approval.
**Impact:** about 62–145 staff hours a year (52 weeks x 2–4 hours a week x 60–70% handled, our assumptions) at $63.41/hour (BLS NJ first-line-supervisor median, x1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $3,931–$9,194 a year.
**Pilot:** 3–4 weeks on live jobs.
