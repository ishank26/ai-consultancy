# Opportunity Audit — Premier Properties of New Brunswick

Roughly 350 units in about a dozen downtown buildings, 25+ years in one city, 2026–27 availability already pre-leasing. The front door is a phone call: prices are listed, then "CALL FOR DETAILS".

## 1. Answer and book, not just ring

**Now (checked November 2026):** a dozen buildings at $1,210–$4,200 under "CALL FOR DETAILS", a form with no phone field, and a 390-pixel phone served a 768-pixel-wide page.

**Would do:** phone-first pages with an availability-and-tour form; address, hours and per-building prices published so search engines and AI assistants quote them right; answers drawn from your own text and AppFolio.

**Impact:** a parent can book on a phone instead of calling back Monday. Each extra 12-month lease is worth $14,520–$50,400 a year at your published rents; a marketplace test measured up to +4.8% scheduled visits.

**Pilot:** 2–3 weeks, five pages.

## 2. An after-hours desk for both leasing waves

**Now:** hours are Monday–Friday 9–5, closed weekends; the written path has no phone field; your $22/hour Leasing Assistant posting bundled calls, applications, tours and deposits in one seat.

**Would do:** text back missed calls and web enquiries, ask unit type, move-in date and a callable number, answer from your FAQ, then offer an open-house slot or a qualification call and log the lead in AppFolio.

**Impact:** about 42–186 staff hours a year (350 units × 2–4 enquiries = 700–1,400; × 12–20 minutes each; × 30–40% automated — our assumptions) at $27.50/hour (your $22 posting × 1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $1,155–$5,115 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, one cluster.

## 3. One price source of truth

**Now:** AppFolio holds units, leases and payments, yet availability is hand-typed on a page marked © 2022, and each applicant's W-9, Preferred Tenant Agreement and $500 deposit is prepared by hand.

**Would do:** a nightly read-only AppFolio pull into one feed driving the site and the answers, so published prices cannot disagree with your system; and an online start where a document agent flags missing items and drafts deposit steps for approval.

**Impact:** about 10–84 staff hours a year (80–240 applications, i.e. 40–120 per wave × 2; × 15–30 minutes each; × 50–70% automated — our assumptions) at $27.50/hour ($22 × 1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $275–$2,310 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, ten packets.

## Why this works

- Works evenings and weekends; deposits go to a person.
- Connects to your Duda site, AppFolio and Google listing.
- Applicant files stay in your screening flow; a person approves what goes out.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot on one cluster.

Prepared from publicly available information. Estimates are illustrative and would be confirmed in a discovery session.
