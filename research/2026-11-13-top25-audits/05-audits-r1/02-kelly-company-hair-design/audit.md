# Opportunity Audit — Kelly & Company Hair Design

A 4.8-star Google rating, nine services from haircuts to lashes, and an August 2026 website rebuild. What a customer meets on the site is thinner than the business behind it.

### 1. Make the front door readable again

**Now (checked 13 Nov 2026):** 11 of 11 booking-page images fail, the header logo 404s, footer links are dead, the home page has an empty title, a "Hello world!" post is live, and Google still says "Claim this business".

**Agent:** real page titles, salon-and-service markup assistants can quote, logo and links repaired, placeholder deleted, an owner-approved answer block (prices, durations, Suite 3), a weekly check against Google.

**Impact:** about 52–156 front-desk hours a year freed up (ASSUMPTION: 52 weeks × 1–3 hours a week × 100% of that time) at $25.03/hour (O*NET NJ receptionist median, x1.25 for benefits) = about $1,301–$3,903 a year.

**Pilot (2 weeks):** before-and-after of what Maps and an assistant answer.

### 2. Answer the phone when you are closed

**Now:** two ways in — the phone and a request form promising a callback; no prices or live availability published; closed Sunday and Monday. Worth checking together: how many calls arrive Sunday–Monday?

**Agent:** voice, text and web intake on your number, answering only from an owner-approved sheet, taking a callback number otherwise, booking and confirming, passing up-dos, colour corrections and complaints to a person with the transcript.

**Impact:** about 208–520 staff hours a year (ASSUMPTION: 52 weeks × 4–10 hours a week × 100% of that time) at $25.03/hour (O*NET NJ receptionist median, x1.25 for benefits) = about $5,205–$13,013 a year.

**Pilot (3 weeks):** one tracked number; calls answered, questions handled, bookings captured.

### 3. Keep the notes from the chair

**Now:** the services page says every treatment starts with a hair analysis, and the page source shows no client-record tool; the one visible complaint is a colour mismatch, not billing.

**Agent:** three staff-only fields after each appointment (formula, service, preferences) and a stylist-approved message before colour, glaze and up-do bookings confirming shade and length; health questions go to a person.

**Impact:** about 8.7–26 hours a year (ASSUMPTION: 52 weeks × 10–30 minutes a week × 100%, rounded down) at $21.73/hour (O*NET NJ hairdresser median, x1.25 for benefits) = about $188–$564 a year.

**Pilot (3–4 weeks):** 60 days of client cards, plus consult messages on colour and up-dos.

## Why this works

- Autonomy: it drafts changes; nothing goes live without your approval.
- Integration: your WordPress site, phone number and Google listing stay as they are.
- Privacy: you approve prices, hours and messages; chair notes stay staff-only.

A 20-minute walkthrough at the salon, then a small paid pilot on one opportunity.
