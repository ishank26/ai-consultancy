# Opportunity Audit — CME Associates (now Artheon)

Artheon — the 2026 combination of CME, Churchill, Naik and Laberge — runs 720-plus engineers from seven offices, and holds East Brunswick's 2026 Township Engineer appointment at up to $200 an hour. Three areas stand out.

## 1. Front door

**Now** (checked November 2026): the contact page gives seven city phone numbers, no form, email, address or hours; no page links to it; "Lorem ipsum" posts are live; cmeusa1.com/contact/ lands on the home page; Google still says CME Associates.

**Agent:** one routed form (project type, town, site, deadline, budget), published email, addresses and hours, a corrected Google listing; each enquiry logged, a reply drafted for an engineer or VP to approve, no fees quoted.

**Impact:** about 104–416 staff hours a year (10–20 enquiries a week x 20–30 minutes each x 60–80% automated, our assumption) at $47.60/hour (NJ median $38.08, sales reps of services, 2025, x1.25 benefits) = about $4,900–$19,800 a year.

**Pilot:** two weeks; one form, seven offices, legacy links redirected.

## 2. Pursuit agent for public bids

**Now:** three Proposal Coordinator postings, duties covering RFQ/RFP submission requirements, compliance matrices and schedules, resume formatting and pursuit logs.

**Agent:** sweeps target portals into a one-page go/no-go brief per notice; on a go it builds the matrix, pulls past proposals and resumes, drafts non-technical sections, flags gaps. Coordinators approve and submit.

**Impact:** about 499–1,310 staff hours a year (3 coordinator roles x 2,080 hours x 20–35% on response production x 40–60% automated — roles from your postings, rest our assumption) at $47.60/hour (NJ median $38.08, 2025, x1.25 benefits) = about $23,700–$62,300 a year.

**Pilot:** three to four weeks; weekly bid digest, two live RFQs.

## 3. Office Engineer assistant

**Now:** six Office Engineer postings cover records and logs — RFIs, submittals, shop drawings, daily, weekly and monthly reports, approvals, minutes — in Excel, Word, Outlook, with Bluebeam, P6 and Procore preferred.

**Agent:** logs each RFI, submittal or field report, checks for missing information, drafts the transmittal and periodic report, tracks ball-in-court dates, escalates overdue items; read-only in the pilot, with the Office Engineer approving each transmittal.

**Impact:** about 936–1,872 staff hours a year (6 roles x 2,080 hours x 15–25% on logs and reports x 50–60% automated — roles from your postings, rest our assumption) at $35.00/hour (NJ median $28.03, bookkeeping clerks, 2025, x1.25 benefits) = about $32,700–$65,500 a year.

**Pilot:** four weeks on one or two live projects.

## Why this works

- **People approve:** no draft reaches a client or agency without an engineer, coordinator or VP sign-off.
- **Your tools:** Excel, Outlook, Bluebeam, P6 and Procore appear in your postings; the site is WordPress.
- **Contained data:** correspondence would sit in a private, client-scoped index, not a public chat tool.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot on the front door.
