# Opportunity Audit — NJP Associates

NJP Associates has advised retirees, families and business owners from one Highland Park office for over 40 years. What we noticed is how much work the free first meeting it advertises asks of whoever answers it.

## 1. A front door a customer can act on

**As a customer sees it** (pages checked for this audit; SEC record updated 18 May 2026): the phone and info@ address are plain text with no tappable link, and the online route is a four-field form with no scheduling or qualifying question. Google still invites you to claim the listing.

**The agent would do:** replace the form with a short qualified intake that offers real first-meeting slots, acknowledge each enquiry with a prep checklist, and claim that listing once you approve the wording.

**Impact:** about 36–108 staff hours a year (5–15 hrs/mo × 12 × 60% handled by the agent, our assumption) at $28.69/hr (CSR median NJ, O\*NET 2024, $47,740 ×1.25 benefits) = about $1,030–$3,090.

**Pilot:** 2–3 weeks — tap-to-call links, qualified intake into a booking calendar, automatic acknowledgement, claimed listing.

## 2. One record for each call, email and form

**As it stands:** no CRM, chat or booking tool is loaded — Google Tag Manager and GA4 only — and Bing lists 8 AM–9 PM weekday hours the firm's own site does not. Worth checking together: where an evening enquiry lands, and what schedules the follow-up?

**The agent would do:** log each enquiry in a light system the firm owns, sort it (new prospect, client servicing, CPA credit request, vendor), draft a reply offering two meeting times for your approval, and set follow-ups at 2, 7 and 21 days.

**Impact:** about 18–48 staff hours a year (3–8 hrs/mo × 12 × 50% handled by the agent, our assumption) at $28.69/hr (same median, ×1.25 for benefits) = about $516–$1,377.

**Pilot:** 3–4 weeks, draft-only at first so Ali Okoh sends each reply.

## 3. Keep the published story matching the record

**As it stands:** the footer calls Norman Politziner an Investment Adviser Representative of Koehler Asset Management; the SEC/FINRA record updated 18 May 2026 says he is not currently registered as one. His bio heading says "Partner", the body president; Google and Bing differ on category and services.

**The agent would do:** index the site, the disclosure text and the regulatory record so staff can ask "what do we say about this?" and get the source cited, then compare published claims with the record weekly and hand you a ranked correction list.

**Impact:** about 12–24 staff hours a year (2–4 hrs/mo × 12 × 50% handled by the agent, our assumption) at $28.69/hr (same median, ×1.25 for benefits) = about $344–$688.

**Pilot:** 3–4 weeks — a 25-question citation test and one live weekly report.

## Why this works

- Nothing is sent, published or booked without your approval.
- It attaches to the form, mailbox, calendar and listing already in use.
- The index covers public pages; no client account data is held.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot on the front door — the cheapest to prove.
