# Opportunity Audit — Menlo Engineering Associates, Inc.

Sixty years and 3,000-plus projects, most recently a 281,215-square-foot Parsippany industrial facility announced in June 2026, but the site does not show it: as of November 2026 the home page has no headline, and Projects and News print page-builder code.

## 1. A front door that works
**Now:** no project or news item shows on either index, the map is an empty frame, the phone number is not tappable, and inquiries arrive with no municipality, lot, timeline or upload.
**Agent:** a home page with headline, six services, address and one CTA, repaired indexes, and an intake that captures scope and uploads, then briefs the project manager.
**Impact:** about 17–64 staff hours a year (3–6 inquiries a week × 15–20 minutes each ÷ 60 × 50–70% automated × 46 weeks; our assumption) at $62.90/hour (O*NET NJ civil engineer median $50.32, 2025, ×1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $1,069–$4,025 a year.
**Pilot:** 3 weeks; before/after count of inquiries and reply time.

## 2. Early notice of new applications
**Now:** work arrives by referral and board appearances (Scott Turner was sworn in as an applicant's expert at Riverdale's Planning Board, November 2025), and two senior roles sit open at $75k–$135k.
**Agent:** daily monitoring of the NJ legal-notices directory and 10–15 towns' agendas into a weekly go/no-go brief, plus proposal drafts and a hearing calendar; a principal approves each.
**Impact:** about 82–220 staff hours a year (6–12 senior hours a week × 30–40% automated × 46 weeks; our assumption) at $62.90/hour (O*NET NJ civil engineer median $50.32, 2025, ×1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $5,157–$13,838 a year.
**Pilot:** 3–4 weeks; one brief and two drafts from your own proposals.

## 3. Review-letter judgement kept in-house
**Now:** the designer posting asks for NJDEP stormwater rules, two principals are stepping back to advisory roles, and each review letter needs a point-by-point answer under N.J.A.C. 7:8. Worth checking together: where do past letters and responses sit?
**Agent:** a private, access-controlled index of your own letters, the rule text and town checklists, drafting one response per comment with citation and closest precedent; a P.E. reviews and seals each draft.
**Impact:** about 24–153 staff hours a year (24–48 review cycles × 4–8 hours × 25–40% draftable; our assumption) at $62.90/hour (O*NET NJ civil engineer median $50.32, 2025, ×1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $1,509–$9,623 a year.
**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks on one live project, with an accuracy log.

## Why this works
- A person approves at every gate: manager for replies, principal for proposals, P.E. for sealed drafts.
- It runs on WordPress, Gravity Forms and email — nothing new to learn.
- Client files stay access-controlled, which a roster of retailers and municipalities warrants.

## Suggested first step
A 20-minute walkthrough of the two broken index pages, then a small paid pilot.
