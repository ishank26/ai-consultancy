# Opportunity Audit — David A. Miller, Attorney (NJ Realty Lawyer)

Thirty years of New Jersey real-estate work, plus wills, probate, business sales and seasonal tax returns. Today a client reaches you by phone or aol.com.

**1. A front door a phone can actually use**
*What's happening now:* Checked 2026-11-13: no form, no input field, no tappable phone link — the "FREE consultation" number sits in a header image. Pages do not resize for a phone, the footer says Manalapan while Google says East Brunswick, and the Google profile is unclaimed (3.0 from 4 reviews).
*What the agent would do:* Claim and complete the Google profile, rebuild five pages with tap-to-dial and one intake form, then sort each enquiry by matter, flag other parties for your conflict check and reply with what to bring.
*Expected impact:* about 52–85 staff hours a year (180–240 enquiries × 25 minutes × 70–85% automated, volumes our assumption) at $37.91/hour (legal secretary median, NJ, 2025, ×1.25 for benefits) = about $1,990–$3,222 a year.
*Pilot:* 2–3 weeks.

**2. Someone answers while you are in a closing**
*What's happening now:* Intake is phone-first and no hours appear on the site. One public reviewer describes two cancelled appointments, told closings take priority. Worth checking together: how many calls arrive mid-closing?
*What the agent would do:* Text back missed calls, ask the same short questions every time, screen for other parties, offer times that respect closing days and contract deadlines, and pass anything unusual to you.
*Expected impact:* about 48–144 staff hours a year (180–360 enquiries × 40 minutes × 40–60% automated, volumes our assumption) at $37.91/hour (legal secretary median, NJ, 2025, ×1.25 for benefits) = about $1,819–$5,458 a year.
*Pilot:* 3–4 weeks.

**3. "Where am I?" without your time**
*What's happening now:* The site shows no portal or status page. You explain the whole transaction at the first meeting, and one reviewer questions whether papers were mailed. Worth checking together: how many status calls each matter generates?
*What the agent would do:* Track each matter on a milestone checklist, tell the client the next step and what to bring, draw answers from that matter's file and your own notes, and escalate anything about fees, advice or the other side.
*Expected impact:* about 20–105 hours a year (160–600 client check-ins × 15 minutes × 50–70% automated, volumes our assumption) at $97.01/hour (lawyer median, NJ, 2025, ×1.25 for benefits) = about $1,940–$10,186 a year.
*Pilot:* 3–4 weeks on residential purchases.

**Why this works**
- Autonomous on routine steps; no advice and no fee quotes — those come to you.
- Sits on your phone, email, calendar and Google listing.
- You approve wording and clear conflict checks; files stay in your accounts.

**Suggested first step:** a 20-minute walkthrough at Suite C7, then a small paid pilot.
