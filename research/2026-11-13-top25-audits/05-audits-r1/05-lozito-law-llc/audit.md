# Opportunity Audit — Lozito Law, LLC

Since 2013 the firm has handled residential and commercial real estate, now with two attorneys, and runs closings remotely by Remote Online Notarization. The site is two pages, no form, no published hours.

## 1. A front door after 5pm

**Now:** As checked in November 2026: two pages, no form, no chat, no published hours, and titles saying Milltown while the address says North Brunswick.

**Agent:** Intake form, tap-to-call links, your hours and a readable address, plus an assistant that acknowledges in seconds and hands both attorneys a one-page summary. No fees, no advice.

**Impact:** 1–3 after-hours enquiries a month (our assumption) × 1 in 3 converting (our assumption) × $1,000–$2,500 NJ flat fee (sourced) = about $4,000–$30,000 a year of closing work.

**Pilot:** two weeks for the page, then the assistant with approval.

## 2. Closing-file coordination

**Now:** No portal, upload, checklist or status page, no practice-management tool: chasing documents and updating buyers, sellers and agents runs through two phone lines and a Gmail inbox.

**Agent:** A per-file checklist that emails the party holding a missing item, chases on a cadence, sends milestone updates from approved templates and proposes signing slots; gaps and at-risk dates go to an attorney.

**Impact:** 15–30 closings a month (our assumption) × 1–3 staff hours per file × that step automated (our assumption) = 15–90 hours a month, 180–1,080 a year, at $37/hour (NJ legal-secretary median $63,080, BLS 2025, ×1.25 for benefits) = about $6,660–$39,960.

**Pilot:** three weeks on two live files with a before/after time log.

## 3. Answers from your own files

**Now:** No FAQ, no document checklist, no "what to bring to closing" — recurring questions get answered live on two phone lines.

**Agent:** Interview both attorneys once and draft the closing and estate-planning playbook for them to sign off; replies quote the passage used or say "not covered — ask the attorney".

**Impact:** 15–40 repeat questions a week (our assumption: 3–8 a day) × 10 minutes each × half answered in one pass (our assumption) = 65–173 hours a year at $37/hour = about $2,405–$6,400.

**Pilot:** two weeks of interviews to a signed playbook, then internal answers.

## Why this works

- Runs itself, with limits: fees, advice and accepting a matter stay human.
- Fits what you use: WordPress, Gmail, the calendar.
- Privacy: messages are approved before sending; work stays in firm-controlled accounts, retention off.

**Worth checking together:** monthly file volume and phone coverage?

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small fixed-fee pilot: contact page and intake assistant first, four weeks.
