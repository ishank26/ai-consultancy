# Opportunity Audit — Deborah A. Rose, P.C.

You are on the November 13, 2026 Middlesex County mediator roster at a published $300 an hour, and directory profiles carry 5.0 client ratings. What a client sees first is different: rosedeb.com no longer loads.

## 1. Put the front door back

**What's happening now (checked 13 November 2026):** rosedeb.com fails to resolve though paid to June 2027; the unclaimed Google profile shows no hours and links there; darosepc.com serves a parked page.

**What the agent would do:** restore the domain, publish five pages with your roster rate and towns served, claim and correct the listing, publish citable facts, and send you a bilingual consultation request to approve.

**Expected impact:** if it recovers 1–2 booked hours a month at your published $300/hour: 1–2 × 12 × $300 = about $3,600–$7,200 a year — our inference, not a measurement.

**Pilot:** two weeks.

## 2. Bilingual intake that answers when you can't

**What's happening now:** the roster lists a phone and fax; there is no online intake, and directory forms route enquiries to the directory. Worth checking together: how many enquiries reach you by phone weekly?

**What the agent would do:** answer calls and web enquiries in English and Spanish, summarise each, note both parties' names for your conflict check, offer three calendar slots, escalate urgent matters, and leave approval of each new matter with you.

**Expected impact:** 1,000–1,500 enquiries a year (our assumption: 4–6 a business day × 250 business days) × 8–15 minutes each × 60–70% automated = about 80–262 attorney hours at $97.50/hour ($78 New Jersey lawyer median, 2025, ×1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $7,800–$25,545 a year.

**Pilot:** three weeks, scored against 30 real enquiries.

## 3. Drafts and status updates from your own files

**What's happening now:** your archived site says the firm is kept small so clients get one-on-one attention, and reviewers praise being kept informed; no portal, upload, calendar or payment page appears.

**What the agent would do:** a private, per-matter assistant over your briefs, letters and the five published appellate decisions would answer with quoted passages and file references, check each citation, and queue drafts for your approval.

**Expected impact:** 50 weeks × 2–4 hours a week × 60–70% automated = about 60–140 attorney hours a year at $97.50/hour = about $5,850–$13,650.

**Pilot:** four weeks, four files, 50 scored questions.

## Why this works

- It sits behind your phone, calendar and email, so how you practise stays the same.
- You approve each new matter, fee quote and outbound message; each step is logged.
- Client files stay in a private, no-training setup with per-matter access, not a public chatbot.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough of the domain and listing, then a small paid two-week pilot.
