# Opportunity Audit — Adway Services Inc

You run a 14-person IT staffing and consulting firm in East Brunswick. On 13 November 2026 the website your Google listing points to returned a Cloudflare error on each page we opened.

## 1. Make the front door reachable

**Now:** On 13 November 2026 your Google listing's website button led to a Cloudflare error page, and 0 of 3 readiness checks passed (findable, answerable, bookable).

**Agent:** A live domain with tappable phone and email, a page per service, a plain-text summary assistants can read, and two intake agents sharing one queue (client requests, résumés). A recruiter approves what goes out.

**Impact:** Reachable again, no headcount change. Build is one-off: 30–60 hours at $45.76/hour (O\*NET NJ web-developer median) = about $1,370–$2,740.

**Pilot:** 2–3 weeks to a live site and two working forms.

## 2. Recruiter assistant for the résumé inbox

**Now:** The last saved careers page shows an "Open Job Requirements" heading but no jobs — the postings are commented out of the HTML — and résumés arrive by email.

**Agent:** It reads each résumé, extracts skills, years, work authorisation and location, removes duplicates and scores candidates against each open role with a one-line reason. A recruiter approves any candidate before a client sees it.

**Impact:** 100–350 staff hours a year (500–1,000 screening hours = 1–2 people × 10 hrs/week × 50 weeks, our assumption, × 20–35% automated); at $48.50/hr (O\*NET NJ HR-specialist median, ×1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $4,850–$16,975.

**Pilot:** 3 weeks on one role family, against your own hours-per-shortlist baseline — worth checking together first.

## 3. Compliance on a deadline

**Now:** Public records show 81 H-1B applications, 9 prevailing-wage requests and 5 PERM filings, placements in eight states, and a wage request dated 11 June 2026.

**Agent:** It drafts posting notices and wage memos from your own filings, keeps one dated ledger per consultant (posting window, public-access file, LCA and wage expiry), and flags what falls due in 30, 60 or 90 days. Counsel signs each filing; it never files with DOL.

**Impact:** 32–105 staff hours a year (8–15 filings × 8–10 hours each, our assumptions, × 50–70% automated); at $48.50/hr (O\*NET NJ HR-specialist median, ×1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $1,550–$5,090.

**Pilot:** 3 weeks: 12 months of filings loaded, ledger built, two notices drafted.

## Why this works

- A named person approves what reaches a client, and each filing.
- It runs on what you already have: email, your domain, your Google listing.
- Work-authorisation and immigration documents stay in your own storage, off any public model endpoint.
