# Opportunity Audit — Amertech Engineering, Inc.

Amertech has engineered land in central New Jersey for decades — your history page counts over 80 years with predecessor firms. Your 2025 public filings are current.

## 1. Make the front door work on a phone

**Today (checked 13 Nov 2026):** the header email link is a placeholder address, the phone number isn't tappable, there's no phone layout, and the footer says "© 2011 – 2016".

**What the agent would do:** rebuild six pages for a phone, fix the email link, publish hours, and add a form that acknowledges and takes plan uploads.

**Impact:** one confirmed contact route restored; no sourced benchmark exists, so no figure.

**Pilot:** two weeks, with a before/after on a phone.

## 2. Sort developer enquiries before an engineer sees them

**Today:** your form asks name, company, address and "product/service" — no parcel, town, project type or deadline. Your 2026 posting carries proposals.

**What the agent would do:** read the enquiry, pull town, parcel, project type and date, reply with a ticket number and the gaps, and book a call.

**Impact:** about 38–96 staff hours a year (48 working weeks x 2–5 hours a week on triage x 40% returned — our assumption) at $62.90/hour (civil engineer median $104,660, NJ, O*NET local wages, x1.25 for benefits — our assumption) = about $2,390–$6,038 a year.

**Pilot:** three weeks, 15–25 live enquiries, PE-approved replies.

## 3. Draft review letters from each town's own rules

**Today:** about ten towns with plans cycling through review rounds — Raritan site plans revised April and August 2025, an Old Bridge plat from July 2025 — so each round means an ordinance read and comments written.

**What the agent would do:** index each town's ordinance and your prior letters; on a resubmission, draft a completeness check, comments citing ordinance sections, and which earlier points were addressed. Your engineer edits and seals.

**Impact:** about 36–144 staff hours a year (48 working weeks x 3–6 letters a week x 1–2 hours drafting x 25% returned — our assumption) at $62.90/hour (as above) = about $2,264–$9,057 a year.

**Pilot:** four weeks, one town, one live application, edit distance measured.

## Why this works

- **Nothing to rip out.** A form, a mailbox, a phone line and a folder are enough; we found no CRM or portal.
- **A person stays in charge.** Every fee, reply and letter is approved, and plans sealed, by your engineer.
- **Your documents stay yours.** Ordinances and plans sit in a private index on your systems.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough of your enquiry path, then a two-week paid pilot on the front door.
