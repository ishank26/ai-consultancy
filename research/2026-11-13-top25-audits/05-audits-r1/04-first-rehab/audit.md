# Opportunity Audit — First Rehab

Two New Jersey clinics — Lakewood and Spotswood — six clinicians, 5.0 from 10 reviews on the Spotswood Google listing. Its website describes Lakewood only.

**1. Give Spotswood a front door**

*Today* (checked November 2026): that link opens a site with no Spotswood page, address, hours or phone, a call button dialling Lakewood, and a form offering Lakewood as the only location.

*What we would build*: one clinic record per site, generating a Spotswood page, structured data and a plain-text hours block; Spotswood added to the form; call buttons routed by page; a weekly mismatch check. Your accounts, your edits.

*Impact*: no dollar figure modelled (no sourced benchmark). Countable today: one of two clinics absent from the site it links to, zero location-specific structured data, a duplicate Google listing, an unclaimed profile. *Pilot*: 2–3 weeks.

**2. An intake agent that answers and books**

*Today*: a form asking for diagnosis and referral source, no availability shown, then a callback; insurance checks routed to the phone.

*What it would do*: collect clinic, insurance and preferred times, then offer real slots; answer missed and after-hours calls without clinical advice, red-flag calls to a person, payer exceptions to the front desk; eligibility as a transaction or a staff-approved draft.

*Impact*: about 40–210 staff hours a year (480–1,200 new patients a year × 10–15 minutes each × 50–70% handled without a callback, our assumptions) at $28.12/hour (Dental Assistant median $22.50, NJ, May 2023, ×1.25 for benefits, our assumption; a proxy) = about $1,120–$5,900 a year. *Pilot*: 2–4 weeks, web and text only.

**3. Keeping a 2–3x/week course on track**

*Today*: your FAQ describes 2–3 visits a week for 4–8 weeks; no booking engine, portal or messaging is visible, and Spotswood's reviews read years old.

*What it would do*: reminders 72 and 24 hours out with the right clinic's address and number; replies turned into real slots; freed hours offered to a waitlist; one review request after discharge to the correct profile.

*Impact*: about 18–202 therapist hours a year (2–5 missed or late-cancelled visits a clinic a week, our assumption, × two clinics × 52 weeks × 1 hour × 9–39% recovered) at $62.50/hour (Physical Therapist median $50.00, NJ, May 2023, ×1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $1,125–$12,625 a year. *Pilot*: 2–4 weeks at Spotswood.

**Why this works**

- Routine steps run unattended; clinical, complaint and payer exceptions reach a person.
- It works on your existing forms, phones and practice calendar.
- Health data under a business associate agreement, role-based access and audit logs.

**Suggested first step**

A 20-minute walkthrough with Baruch Gottlieb, DPT, Clinical Director, then a small paid pilot. Worth checking together: your monthly new-patient requests and missed visits?
