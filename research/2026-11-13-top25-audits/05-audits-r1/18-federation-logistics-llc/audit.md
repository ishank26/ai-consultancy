# Opportunity Audit — Federation Logistics, LLC

Federation Logistics runs food-grade, bonded and liquor-licensed warehousing and port drayage from North Brunswick, with four New Jersey sites and 500,000-plus square feet claimed. Front door: a phone number hidden on phones, one long survey form that sends no reply.

### 1. A front door that works on a phone

**What's happening now** (checked November 2026): About us, Services and Contact go to "#", so no service pages exist; hours cover 42.5 of the week's 168; below 1439px the call button is hidden.

**What the agent would do** One page per service, working navigation, one hours block, tap-to-call and an assistant answering only from approved copy.

**Expected impact** A buyer checking after hours finds no service page, no call button and three closing times; no dollar figure is claimed.

**Pilot** Two to three weeks, measured on form starts and calls.

### 2. Quote requests answered, then chased

**What's happening now** The only online quote path is a form headed "Customer Survey" of about 70 fields — monthly volumes, pallet grades, EDI mix — posting to quote.php with no acknowledgement and no CRM behind it.

**What the agent would do** Reply at once with a reference, put the free text into a fixed request sheet, ask only what is missing, flag bonded or licence work, chase at 2/5/10 days; a person approves each rate.

**Expected impact** about 40–416 staff hours a year (8–25 requests a week × 10–25 minutes × 60–80% automated × 50 weeks, our assumption) at $27.31/hour (NJ clerk median $21.85 × 1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $1,092–$11,362 a year.

**Pilot** Three to four weeks.

### 3. Answers on bond and licence paperwork

**What's happening now** The site lists FDA, UIIA, bonded, SQF and ABC scope but publishes no upload or document-request route — info@ is the only document channel — against 15-day and 10-working-day filing clocks.

**What the agent would do** Answer from your own procedures with citations, draft missing-document requests, build a checklist per shipment or renewal, run deadline clocks as fixed rules; your compliance owner approves every filing instruction.

**Expected impact** about 22–133 staff hours a year (3–10 requests a week × 15–20 minutes × 60–80% automated × 50 weeks, our assumption) at $28.69/hour (NJ customer service median $22.95 × 1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $631–$3,815 a year.

**Pilot** Four weeks on one scope.

**Why this works**
- A person approves every rate, answer and filing instruction.
- It sits on the quote form, the info@ mailbox and a calendar, not in place of a warehouse system.
- Client volumes and customs documents stay private, with a log of what was sent to whom.

**Suggested first step** A 20-minute walkthrough on a phone, then a small paid pilot.

Prepared from publicly available information. Estimates are illustrative and would be confirmed in a discovery session.
