# Opportunity Audit — B. Nathaniel Bespoke

You are a custom clothier and alterations shop at 28 Paterson St in New Brunswick, selling suits, wedding packages and alterations, with 4.8 from 497 reviews. The tailoring is well reviewed; the way in is a free-text form or a phone call.

## 1. One front door, one set of facts

**Now:** "Book Now" opens a free-text form with no slots, prices or confirmation (checked 13 Nov 2026); weekday opening shows as 9:00am, 9:30am and 10:00am in three places.

**Agent:** keeps one approved record of phone, hours and services, adds a scheduler, and checks the review profile and directories weekly.

**Impact:** about 156–312 staff hours a year (15–30 inquiry calls a week x 20 minutes each x 60% handled — our assumption) at $25.03/hour ($20.02 NJ receptionist median, 2025, x1.25 for benefits — our assumption) = about $3,904–$7,809 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–3 weeks; scheduler live.

## 2. Fittings booked without phone tag

**Now:** your site says staff call customers for the first fitting; home and office visits add travel, and enquiries land in a Gmail inbox.

**Agent:** watches the form, missed calls and inbox, sorts requests, offers slots, books, reminds and re-offers; prices outside your bands go to a person.

**Impact:** about 260–624 staff hours a year (20–32 appointments a week x 30–45 minutes each x 50% handled — our assumption) at $25.03/hour ($20.02 NJ receptionist median, 2025, x1.25 for benefits — our assumption) = about $6,507–$15,618 a year.

**Pilot:** 3–4 weeks, one service line.

## 3. Answering "how much and how soon"

**Now:** the site lists no prices and the alterations page no rates; starting prices appear on the older site ($495 suits, $595 tuxedos), and the FAQs link shows no questions.

**Agent:** replies with your signed-off price band and a ready date after two questions, hands off rush, gown and bulk work, and drafts the FAQ page.

**Impact:** about 104–260 staff hours a year (12–20 price questions a week x 20 minutes each x 50–75% handled — our assumption) at $25.94/hour ($20.75 NJ tailor and dressmaker median, 2025, x1.25 for benefits — our assumption) = about $2,697–$6,744 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–3 weeks: bands for your busiest services.

## Why this works

- It runs on its own: checks, reminders and re-offers happen without anyone minding them; exceptions queue for a person.
- It uses your tools — the form, Gmail, your calendar — plus a simple ticket board.
- You approve any price, promise or listing change before it goes out; customer data stays yours.

Worth checking together: which phone number and hours are the true ones?

**Suggested first step:** a 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot.
