# Opportunity Audit — Midlantic Property Management

Midlantic has managed New Jersey community associations since 1995 from Highland Park and Long Branch, plus a Little Egg Harbor field office. Online, a board finds a free Weebly site stamped 2013 and mpm-nj.com showing a GoDaddy parking page (checked 13 November 2026).

**1. A front door that works after 4:30 p.m.**
*Now:* proposals arrive by phone or a hand-typed 11-field email; work orders and emergencies share one number. No form, no tap-to-call link; hours end 4:30. The Google profile is unclaimed, 1.6 stars, no hours.
*The agent:* proposal and resident request forms capturing those same fields, a claimed profile with hours, and a machine-readable site on the owned domain. Proposals wait for your approval.
*Impact:* a board can ask at 9 p.m. and land in a record — about 130–291 staff hours a year (5–8 hours a week x 52 x 50%–70% automated — our assumption) at $30.20/hour (O*NET Secretaries and Administrative Assistants median, NJ, 2026, x1.25 for benefits — our assumption) = about $3,926–$8,788 a year.
*Pilot:* 2–3 weeks.

**2. Work-order triage with a receipt for the resident**
*Now:* residents phone (732) 828-7200 or email a free-text template; the firm says after-hours calls reach a live operator who takes the facts and arranges dispatch.
*The agent:* reads each request, applies written emergency criteria, de-duplicates, opens a ticket, acknowledges the resident, routes routine work to the manager. Exceptions go to a person.
*Impact:* about 120–672 staff hours a year (20–80 hours a month x 12 x 50%–70% recovered — our assumption) at $30.20/hour (O*NET Secretaries and Administrative Assistants median, NJ, 2026, x1.25 for benefits — our assumption) = about $3,624–$20,294 a year.
*Pilot:* 3–4 weeks on two associations.

**3. Delinquency letters that end with a human signature**
*Now:* the FAQ sets out monthly assessments, a 30-day delinquency stage, interest and late charges, lien notices and possible foreclosure, run from a shared back office of AP, AR, accounting and community admin roles.
*The agent:* recomputes balances, applies each association's own collection policy, drafts the stage letter, stops while a dispute is open. Fee corrections and lien referrals wait for a named person.
*Impact:* about 96–288 staff hours a year (20–40 hours a month x 12 x 40%–60% drafted — our assumption) at $35.04/hour (O*NET Bookkeeping, Accounting and Auditing Clerks median, NJ, 2026, x1.25 for benefits — our assumption) = about $3,363–$10,091 a year.
*Pilot:* 3–4 weeks replaying one association's payments.

## Why this works
- Nothing leaves on its own: proposals, prices, fee corrections and lien referrals wait for a named person's sign-off.
- It sits in front of your phone, email and accounting routines rather than replacing them.
- Resident names, addresses and ledgers stay in one place, staff-only, with a log of who saw what.

**Suggested first step:** a 20-minute walkthrough at 315 Raritan Avenue, then a small paid pilot on one association.
