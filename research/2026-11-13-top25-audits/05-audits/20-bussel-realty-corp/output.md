# Opportunity Audit — Bussel Realty Corp

BRC has marketed industrial and office space across Central and North Jersey since 1984, and says it handles over three million square feet a year. Your New Brunswick pages point to the Edison suite, so we looked at the public front door as a whole.

## 1. The front door today

What a customer sees (checked 13 November 2026): the contact page is a 23-broker directory with the main number in plain text — no form, no booking, no tap-to-call — and `/contact-us/` returned an IIS 404. Listings read "[-address on file-]" and search results load only in a browser.

**What the agent would do:** with a developer, fix the error and contact pages, make the numbers tappable, add machine-readable business details, and publish each listing as a page search engines can read — with a person approving any address release.

**Impact:** no dollar figure here, because we have no benchmark. Plainly: a caller gets through in one tap, and a search engine or AI assistant sees inventory rather than a placeholder.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, ten live listings rendered and readable.

## 2. Enquiry intake and routing

Enquiries reach 23 broker mobiles or one admin extension, and the only structured form is seller-side. Worth checking together: how many arrive after hours, and who replies first?

**What the agent would do:** capture type, size, location, budget and timing, route each enquiry to the best-fit broker, and follow up at 24 and 72 hours; brokers confirm every tour time and address release.

**Impact:** 4 hours a week × 52 weeks × 100% handled = 208 hours, up to 8 × 52 × 100% = 416 staff hours a year (volume and hours are our assumption), at $30/hour ($24/hour O*NET median for secretaries and administrative assistants, NJ, × 1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $6,240–$12,480 a year.

**Pilot:** one county, two weeks, against a two-week call baseline.

## 3. Collateral and market reports

You are hiring a full-time marketing/admin assistant at $42,000–$50,000 for brochures, flyers, research, social posts and press, while the Quarterly Report category renders empty.

**What the agent would do:** draft each pack from one listing record — flyer, web summary, LinkedIn post, deal release, quarterly snapshot — for approval.

**Impact:** 40 hours a week × 52 weeks × 25% of the role = 520 hours, up to 40 × 52 × 50% = 1,040 staff hours a year (our assumption), at $30/hour = about $15,600–$31,200 a year.

**Pilot:** 8–10 listings and one Middlesex snapshot.

## Why this works

- A person approves every address release, tour time and published pack.
- It works with the existing listing database and broker CRM login.
- Enquiries and publish events are logged and reviewable.

A 20-minute walkthrough of these findings, then a small paid pilot.
