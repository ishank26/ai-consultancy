# Opportunity Audit — Yuhas Plumbing & Heating

Thirty-five years at 33 Jackson St, licensed plumbing #7275 and HVAC #147600, a Carrier Factory Authorized Dealer. What a customer meets first is thinner: a phone line, a Gmail address and call buttons that don't connect.

## 1. Three call buttons, two wrong numbers

**Today** (checked 13 Nov 2026): Google Maps reads "Claim this business", with no hours or website link. Two of the three site call buttons dial wrong numbers; /contact returns 404.

**The agent would** claim the Google and Nextdoor profiles with weekday hours and the site link, make each number tap-to-call, add a short quote form routing urgent jobs to (908) 482-8296, and request reviews after jobs.

**Impact** anyone tapping a broken button reaches a dead or wrong number, and requests outside 9–6 weekdays have no route in. Call volume isn't public.

**Pilot** profile claimed, links fixed, form live in weeks 1–2.

## 2. A line that answers when the crew can't

**Today** hours exist only on third-party listings: Monday–Friday 9–6, weekends closed, and no published after-hours route for the emergency number.

**The agent would** answer missed and after-hours calls and messages, ask a few set questions, alert the on-call tech, book routine work into real windows, and text a confirmation. The owner approves each emergency dispatch.

**Impact** 52 weeks x 20 hours/week (our assumption) x 25–50% automated (our assumption) = about 260–520 staff hours a year at $32.81/hour (dispatcher median, NJ, May 2025, x1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $8,500–$17,000 a year.

**Pilot** one number forwarded after hours, every call reviewed.

## 3. Estimates and invoices that stop sitting

**Today** no scheduling, invoicing or customer-record tooling is visible publicly; contact is by Gmail. One Google review describes a reasonable-looking estimate cancelled after a question.

**The agent would** take the tech's photos and voice note, draft the estimate for owner approval, follow up on day 2 and day 7, then draft the invoice and reminders.

**Impact** 52 weeks x 1.5–4.5 hours/week (our assumption) x 100% automated (our assumption) = about 78–234 staff hours a year at $47.02/hour (plumber median, NJ, May 2025, x1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $3,600–$11,000 a year.

**Pilot** one job type end to end for a month.

## Why this works

- A person approves each price, dispatch and review reply.
- It sits in front of your existing numbers and Gmail inbox.
- Recording is off by default; transcripts follow a policy.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough at 33 Jackson St, then a small paid pilot on one number after hours.
