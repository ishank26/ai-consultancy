# Opportunity Audit — Stone Ridge Engineering, LLC

You have built a principal-led MEP/FP practice on referrals: 12+ years, a P.E. and an NJ electrical licence. Seen as a prospective client would, your website and Google listing are not taking new work.

## 1. A contact form that cannot send

The contact page shows five fields and a Submit button, but its action is `javascript:void(0)` and its jQuery is blocked, so submitting does nothing. At 390 px the menu button opens nothing and the phone number is not tappable.

The agent would rebuild the form on a real endpoint with a confirmation screen and an alert to you, add tap-to-call, and repair the menu.

No dollar figure: no analytics or call tracking exists to size today's traffic.

Pilot: 2–3 weeks. Worth checking together: how many enquiries arrive by web versus phone?

## 2. Enquiry-to-proposal drafting

Enquiries arrive as a call or a free-text comment with no questions on building type, size, systems or deadline, while each deliverable needs scoping plus a written proposal.

The agent asks your qualifying questions, sorts the request into a service line, drafts in your template and queues 3/7/14-day follow-ups. You set each fee; nothing sends without you.

about 48–307 staff hours a year (48–96 proposals × 4–8 hours scoping and writing each × 25–40% automated; the proposal count, hours each and one principal doing the work are our assumptions) at $62.90/hour (O*NET NJ civil engineer median $50.32, ×1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $3,019–$19,310 a year.

Pilot: one service line, due diligence first.

## 3. Private search over your own reports

Due diligence reports, feasibility studies and peer reviews are reusable documents, but the site shows no portal or file-transfer tool.

The agent indexes your own reports and standard details, answering with a citation or "not found" rather than a guess, and assembles draft skeletons marked unverified.

about 24–104 staff hours a year (48–52 weeks × 2–5 hours a week hunting prior reports and re-assembling boilerplate × 25–40% automated; the weekly hours are our assumption) at $62.90/hour (same median, ×1.25 benefits) = about $1,509–$6,541 a year.

Pilot: 12–24 months of one client's reports and 20 test questions you score.

## Why this works

- **You stay the engineer:** each fee, scope and conclusion is yours; nothing is signed or sealed by software.
- **Data stays yours:** one dedicated index, per-client separation, no client PDFs in consumer chat tools.
- **The first fix needs no new subscriptions.**

**Suggested first step:** a 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot on the front door.
