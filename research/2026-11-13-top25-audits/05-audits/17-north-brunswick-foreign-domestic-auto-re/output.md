# Opportunity Audit — North Brunswick Foreign & Domestic Auto Repairs

You're a 4.5-star shop inside a bp station at 590 Georges Rd, with 47 Google reviews. Checked 13 November 2026, your listing shows an "Add website" prompt, so a customer who won't phone has nowhere to go.

**1. A front door open when the shop isn't**
Today (13 Nov 2026): hours posted, an "Add website" prompt, no booking, quote or message button; Birdeye's copy unclaimed.
The agent builds one plain page (services, makes, hours, languages, area, pricing policy, tap-to-call), a booking form feeding #2's queue, and directory corrections you approve.
Impact: a Sunday searcher finds a shop they can book. About 26–195 staff hours a year (25–75 calls a week, 3–5 minutes each, 40–60% automated, our assumption) at $34.75/hour ($27.80 New Jersey OES 2023 mean wage, automotive service technicians and mechanics, x1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $903–$6,776.
Pilot: 2–3 weeks, page live with form and automatic text-back, Birdeye claimed.

**2. The phone answered while you're under a hood**
Today: price and status questions land on one number; pricing is the most-tagged topic, at 12 reviews.
The agent handles texts and web chat: year/make/model, symptom, a price menu you sign off, bay-aware slots, booking, status notes; off-menu questions reach you with a drafted reply.
Impact: About 14–156 staff hours a year (18–60 inquiries a week, 3–5 minutes each, 30–60% automated, our assumption) at $34.75/hour ($27.80 New Jersey OES 2023 mean wage, automotive service technicians and mechanics, x1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $486–$5,421.
Pilot: 3–4 weeks, 20–50 real conversations reviewed weekly.

**3. One job record, and follow-up that doesn't rely on memory**
Today: card payment in person, no invoice portal, and one 1-star review saying a car billed over $2,000 wouldn't start.
The agent writes the job file (estimate, text approval, invoice, payment on your card processor, review request, next-service reminder) and drafts review replies for you.
Impact: About 13–72 staff hours a year (0.5–2 admin hours a week x 52 = 26–104 hours, 50–70% agent-handled, our assumption) at $34.75/hour ($27.80 New Jersey OES 2023 mean wage, automotive service technicians and mechanics, x1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $451–$2,502.
Pilot: 2–4 weeks, including a drafted reply to that review.

**Why this works**
- Nothing leaves without you: off-menu questions, disputes and review replies arrive as a draft.
- It runs on your phone number, card processor and Google listing; records are access-limited, with retention you set.

Worth checking together: how many calls arrive mid-job?

**Suggested first step**
A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot measured on calls answered.
