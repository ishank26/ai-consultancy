# Opportunity Audit — The Law Office of Eileen M. Foley, Esq.

A matrimonial practice here since the late 1980s, handling every matter start to finish yourself, with a 2024 county bar Pro Bono Award. Since referrals bring most clients, we checked what a referral sees.

## 1. Your front door

**What's happening now** (checked 13 November 2026): the secure site returns a certificate warning, there is no enquiry form or tap-to-call link, and Google advertises "Online appointments" with nothing to book through.

**What the agent would do**: fix the certificate and force https, add tap-to-call and a consultation form, publish address, hours and practice areas as structured data, and keep Google, Avvo, Lawyers.com and Yelp matching Suite W21.

**Expected impact**: about 12–36 hours a year (12 months × 1–3 hours a month of repeat questions × 100% handled; our assumption) at $37.90/hour (O*NET NJ legal-secretary median $63,080/yr, ×1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $454–$1,364 a year.

**Pilot**: two weeks, tested on a phone.

## 2. Answering when you are in court

**What's happening now**: enquiries arrive by phone, Monday–Friday 9–5, or email — no form, scheduler or after-hours message — and each begins with a free 15-minute call from you. Some matters are urgent.

**What the agent would do**: take structured details from a form, email or missed call, pre-check the other party against your matter list, escalate urgent contacts to you, and book only after your approval.

**Expected impact**: 48 weeks × 2–5 hours a week of attorney and assistant time (our assumption) × 100% of first contact = 96–240 hours a year at $37.90/hour (O*NET NJ legal-secretary median, ×1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $3,638–$9,096 a year.

**Worth checking together**: how many enquiries arrive after hours?

**Pilot**: three to four weeks, form and email first.

## 3. Paperwork that starts itself

**What's happening now**: no client portal, upload, e-signature or payment link, and fax is the only transaction channel — so engagement letters, retainer invoices and checklists are assembled by hand.

**What the agent would do**: draft the engagement letter from your template, raise the retainer invoice at your amount, request e-signature, issue a trust-compliant payment link (amounts never agent-decided), and serve a checklist on a secure upload page.

**Expected impact**: 48 weeks × 1.5–4 hours a week × 90% automated (our assumption) = 64–172 hours a year at $42.97/hour (O*NET NJ paralegal median $71,500/yr, ×1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $2,750–$7,390 a year.

**Pilot**: three to four weeks on divorce files, every document approved by you.

## Why this works
- Agent drafts and routes; you approve every booking, document and listing change.
- It runs on what you already use — host, Word templates, calendar, email.
- Client details stay in an account you control; no legal advice, no fee beyond your policy.

## Suggested first step
A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid two-week front-door pilot.
