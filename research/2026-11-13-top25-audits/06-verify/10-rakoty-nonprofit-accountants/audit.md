# Opportunity Audit — Rakoty Nonprofit Accountants

Your site sets out a clear nonprofit practice: bookkeeping, grant reporting, audit leadership, 990s, with about two dozen client logos. On 13 November 2026 a visitor's first screen was a percentage loader stuck at 9–32%, with nothing behind it.

## 1. Repair the front door, then add an intake agent

**Now:** the "Schedule an appointment" button leads to a "not found" page, nav buttons have no link, and no contact form renders; enquiries come by phone or email.

**What it does:** the build fixes the loader and speed, restores booking, adds a short form and tap-to-call, and publishes address, hours and phone; it then drafts each enquiry's reply and call slots for a person to send.

**Impact:** visitors from ads and search can read the offer and ask for a call. Our assumption of 2–6 enquiries a month, 1–2 converting at your published $99/month = about $1,180–$2,370 a year of recurring revenue.

**Pilot:** 2–3 weeks, with before-and-after phone-width screenshots.

## 2. An agent that chases documents and deadlines

**Now:** your nonprofit page lists close by the 10th, quarterly grant budget-to-actual, audit lists, 990s and 1099/W-2 season, and each starts with an email chasing a missing statement.

**What it does:** it builds a request list per engagement, sends specific asks, chases on a fixed cadence, files what arrives, and leaves a missing-items list each morning.

**Impact:** about 86–345 staff hours a year (our assumption: 24 clients x 1–3 hours chasing per close x 12 closes x 30–40% agent-handled) at $35/hour (bookkeeping clerk median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $3,000–$12,000 a year.

**Pilot:** 3–4 weeks on 3–5 volunteer clients, measured against last month's hours.

## 3. A grant and 990 answer desk

**Now:** training and support is a listed service, and your blog already covers 990s, cost allocation and accrual basis. Worth checking together: how much of a week goes to repeating those answers?

**What it does:** it answers from your articles, checklists and client agreements with a citation, sends judgement questions to the Client Manager, and keeps each client's documents separate.

**Impact:** about 43–172 staff hours a year (our assumption: 24 clients x 0.5–1.5 hours of routine questions per client per month x 12 months x 30–40% agent-handled) at $60/hour (accountant median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $2,580–$10,320 a year.

**Pilot:** 3–4 weeks, scored on 30 real past questions by a preparer.

## Why this works

- A person approves each outbound reply and grant submission.
- It sits beside WordPress, Tag Manager, QuickBooks and email.
- Client tax data stays in your own systems, under your retention rules.

A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot in one area.
