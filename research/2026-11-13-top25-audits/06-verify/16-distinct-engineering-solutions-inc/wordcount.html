# Opportunity Audit — Distinct Engineering Solutions, Inc. (DESI)

Thirty-plus staff, seven service lines, an accredited lab, public-agency clients in four states — and one generic contact form behind it all (site and listings read November 2026).

### 1. One front door, sorted by service line

**Now:** a six-field form with no service picker, no booking link and no address on the contact page; inspection, hazmat and geotechnical requests share one inbox (checked November 2026).

**Agent would:** ask four to six scoping questions per line, check service area and prequalifications, reply with the documents needed and book a call with the right project manager. Priced quotes go to a person.

**Impact:** about 50–100 staff hours a year (100–200 enquiries × 1 hour × 50% automated, our assumption) at $28.69/hour (customer-service median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $1,434–$2,869 a year.

**Pilot:** three weeks, three lines, judged on time to first reply.

### 2. Inspection scheduling and report chasing

**Now:** your careers page plans 14-plus hires on workload and backlog, including four NICET-certified inspectors, and a posted Scheduling Coordinator role covers inspectors' daily schedules and report tracking.

**Agent would:** draft a daily plan matching each job's required certification to inspector credentials, send confirmations, flag conflicts, then chase missing field data and late reports. Confirmed changes stay with your coordinator.

**Impact:** about 150–225 staff hours a year (250 working days × 2–3 hours a day × 30% automated, our assumption) at $28.69/hour (customer-service median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $4,303–$6,455 a year.

**Pilot:** four weeks on special inspection — certification matrix, draft schedule, report board.

### 3. Bid discovery and proposal compliance

**Now:** your Proposal Coordinator posting covers RFP requirement review, deadline tracking, content-library upkeep and portal submissions, against prequalifications in four states.

**Agent would:** scan your agency portals daily, post a one-page go/no-go brief, then build the compliance matrix, an addendum calendar and a first draft of the firm-background sections; a person decides, prices and submits.

**Impact:** about 144–240 staff hours a year (30–50 submissions × 12 hours × 40% automated, our assumption) at $28.69/hour (customer-service median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $4,131–$6,885 a year.

**Pilot:** four weeks, three portals watched, one live proposal end to end.

### Why this works

- It runs on what you already have: your website form, inboxes and calendars.
- A person approves every quote, confirmed schedule change and bid submission; the agent drafts and routes.
- Project documents stay in your own accounts, with an audit trail.

### Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough of the front door, then a small paid three-week pilot — worth checking together: how many hours a week scheduling and chasing take?
