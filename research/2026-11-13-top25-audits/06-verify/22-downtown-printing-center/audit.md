# Opportunity Audit — Downtown Printing Center

Downtown Printing Center has printed at 46 Paterson St. since 1977 — digital, offset, apparel, signs, counter services. The website is a bare four-page site marked © 2022, with no services, hours or prices as readable text.

## 1. Fix the front door

**Now (checked Nov 2026):** a logo, one stock press photo, a © 2022 footer; intake is a Name/Email/Message box with no size, quantity or artwork, and hours differ across listings.

**The agent:** readable product pages, a structured quote form, one set of hours, review replies for your approval.

**Impact:** about 40–150 staff hours a year (50 weeks x 1–3 hours a week x 80–100% automated; our assumption on staffing) at $28.75/hour (O*NET Customer Service Rep median, NJ, x1.25 benefits) = about $1,150–$4,312 a year.

**Pilot:** two to three weeks — three product pages, live quote form, hours corrected.

## 2. Add a quote desk

**Now:** quotes start from a call or a message like "flyers for Friday", on a counter that also runs money transfers and passport photos. Worth checking together: how much of the day goes on chasing sizes and artwork?

**The agent:** reads each request, fills in size, quantity, stock, deadline and artwork, drafts a price from your past jobs, opens a ticket; you approve prices.

**Impact:** about 37–160 staff hours a year (50 weeks x 3–8 hours a week x 25–40% automated; our assumption on staffing) at $28.75/hour (O*NET Customer Service Rep median, NJ, x1.25 benefits) = about $1,063–$4,600 a year.

**Pilot:** three to four weeks in shadow mode on 30–50 past jobs.

## 3. Answer questions on tap

**Now:** we found no published prices or samples, and four families each carry different stocks; a 2016 state order records a regulated line lost over verification failures.

**The agent:** reads your price lists and past quotes, answers with the source, says when something isn't in the documents, offers only approved answers.

**Impact:** about 17–33 staff hours a year (20–40 questions a week x 2 minutes x 50% handled, over 50 weeks; our assumption on volume) at $28.75/hour (O*NET Customer Service Rep median, NJ, x1.25 benefits) = about $488–$948 a year.

**Pilot:** three weeks over 100–300 pages, tested on 40 counter questions.

## Why this works

- **Autonomy with limits:** the agents draft and follow up; they don't commit prices or deadlines they can't verify.
- **Your tools, your approval, your data:** the form feeds a queue, prices and pages wait for your sign-off, documents stay yours.

**Suggested first step:** a 20-minute walkthrough at the counter, then a small paid pilot.

Prepared from publicly available information. Estimates are illustrative and would be confirmed in a discovery session.

## For the founder (not part of the audit)

**Email draft:** I looked at print shops in downtown New Brunswick and your website is easy to like and hard to use — the home page is a logo and a press photo, and the form asks for a message rather than a quantity or a file. A customer can't see what you print, get any sense of price, or send artwork. If it's useful, I'd take twenty minutes at the counter to show you what a rebuilt front door and an automatic quote assistant would look like.

**Walk-in opener:** I build software that answers and prices quote requests for local businesses — could I show you one thing your website is doing that loses you jobs?
