# Opportunity Audit — Law Office of Mark F. Hughes III

The online front door is one free Weebly page from 2015, offering DWI and refusal work from suites in East Brunswick and Little Silver. Its "CALL NOW" button does nothing when tapped, and the printed number is the only way in.

## 1. A front door that works on a phone

**Now** (checked November 2026): no form, email, hours or booking and nothing structured for search assistants; one page, last modified 21 May 2015. Worth checking together: where after-hours calls land?

**The agent would:** build four to six pages on your own domain, one per charge, naming the courts you appear in, with hours, tap-to-call, a form to one inbox and facts assistants read.

**Impact:** about 104–208 clerical hours a year (52 weeks x 2–4 hours x the full clerical share, our assumption) at $37.91/hour (legal-secretary median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $3,942–$7,885 a year.

**Pilot:** two to four weeks, live, with a baseline of enquiries.

## 2. A deadline and court-date clerk

**Now:** the attorney's name and ID (021041994) appear on New Jersey's administrative ineligibility lists in 2024, 2025 and the current list to 9 November 2026 — compliance lists, not misconduct findings, but a listed attorney cannot practise.

**The agent would:** keep one register of dated obligations with 60/30/7-day reminders, pre-fill the paperwork for you to sign, watch the published lists and merge forwarded court notices into one calendar.

**Impact:** about 16–40 deadline hours a year (40 dated items x 0.4–1.0 hours x the full tracking share, our assumption) at $37.91/hour (legal-secretary median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $606–$1,516 a year.

**Pilot:** two to four weeks, starting with the remediation steps.

## 3. An after-hours intake line

**Now:** intake is one printed number, with no hours, no voicemail-to-email, no form and no scheduler.

**The agent would:** answer after-hours texts and web enquiries, take charge, county and court date, reply from your written FAQ, offer two slots and escalate custody or same-day matters to you.

**Impact:** about 60–180 attorney hours a year (12 months x 5–15 hours x the full routine-capture share, our assumption) at $97.01/hour (lawyer median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 for benefits) = about $5,820–$17,461 a year.

**Pilot:** two to four weeks on text and web, logged.

## Why this works
- Nothing is filed, quoted or sent without your sign-off.
- It fits what you already use: a phone, an inbox, a calendar.
- Callers describe pending charges, so data sits on zero-retention or private endpoints.

## Suggested first step
A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot on the deadline register.
