# Opportunity Audit — North Brunswick Foreign & Domestic Auto Repairs

You're rated 4.5 stars from 47 Google reviews, an independent shop inside a bp station on Georges Rd, with reviewers crediting honesty and fair pricing. Checked 13 November 2026, your Google listing shows an "Add website" prompt and no booking, quote or message option.

**1. A front door open when the shop isn't**
Now: hours and a phone number, an "Add website" prompt, Birdeye's copy unclaimed.
The agent: one plain page (services, makes, hours, area, pricing, tap-to-call) with a booking form feeding #2, Birdeye claimed, directory fixes drafted for approval.
Impact: a Sunday searcher can book. About 6–31 staff hours a year (5–15 after-hours calls a week × 52 weeks × 4 minutes each × 40–60% automated, our assumption) at $34.75/hour ($27.80 NJ OES 2023 mean wage, automotive technicians, x1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $208–$1,077 a year.
Pilot: 2–3 weeks; page live, Birdeye claimed.

**2. The phone covered while you're under a hood**
Now: one contact number, and pricing is the most-tagged review topic (12 reviews; "fair pricing" on 6).
Worth checking together: how many calls arrive mid-bay, and how long they take?
The agent: answers texts and web chat — year/make/model, symptom, a price menu you sign off, slots, status — and escalates the rest with a drafted reply.
Impact: about 18–124 staff hours a year (18–60 enquiries a week × 52 weeks × 4 minutes each × 30–60% from your menu, our assumption) at $34.75/hour (NJ OES 2023 mean wage, x1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $625–$4,309 a year.
Pilot: 3–4 weeks, 20–50 conversations reviewed weekly.

**3. One job record instead of follow-up by memory**
Now: payment at the counter, no online invoice path, and one of four 1-star reviews says a $2,000-plus bill left a car not starting.
The agent: writes the job file — estimate, approval, invoice, payment recorded, review request, reminder — and drafts replies.
Impact: about 13–72 staff hours a year (0.5–2 admin hours a week × 52 weeks × 50–70% agent-handled, our assumption) at $34.75/hour (NJ OES 2023 mean wage, x1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $451–$2,502 a year.
Pilot: 2–4 weeks, including a drafted reply to that review.

**Why this works**
- Drafts, not sends: off-menu questions, disputes and replies wait for your approval.
- It sits on what you already have: your phone number and listing.
- Records are access-limited, store no card numbers, keep details as long as you set.

**Suggested first step**
A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot measured on calls answered.
