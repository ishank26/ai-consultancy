# Opportunity Audit — Artheon (formerly CME Associates)

Artheon, the 2026 combination of CME, Churchill, Naik and Laberge, reports more than 720 professionals across seven offices, and holds East Brunswick's 2026 Township Engineer appointment. Three areas stand out.

## 1. Front door

**Now** (checked November 2026): the contact page gives seven city phone numbers and no form, email, address or hours; nothing on the home page links to it; "Lorem ipsum" posts are live; Google still says CME Associates.

**Agent:** one routed form, a published email, addresses and hours, a corrected Google listing; each enquiry logged, a reply drafted for an engineer or VP to approve.

**Impact:** about 104–416 hours a year (10–20 enquiries a week x 20–30 minutes x 60–80% automated; inputs our assumption) at $28.68/hour (customer service reps, NJ median $22.95, 2025, x1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $2,980–$11,930 a year.

**Pilot:** two weeks; one form, seven offices, redirects.

## 2. Bid pursuit assistant

**Now:** the careers board carries three Proposal Coordinator postings; the posting covers reviewing RFQs and RFPs for submission requirements, compliance matrices, resume formatting and pursuit logs.

**Agent:** sweeps target portals into a one-page go/no-go brief per notice; on a go it builds the matrix, pulls past proposals and resumes, drafts non-technical sections, flags gaps; coordinators approve and submit.

**Impact:** about 499–1,310 hours a year (3 posted coordinator roles x 2,080 hours x 20–35% on response production x 40–60% automated; roles from your postings, rest our assumption) at $30.04–$45.07/hour (the $50,000–$75,000 a year in your posting, x1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $14,980–$59,040 a year.

**Pilot:** three weeks; weekly digest, two live RFQs.

## 3. Office Engineer assistant

**Now:** six Office Engineer postings are open; the posting covers project records and logs, RFI, submittal and shop-drawing tracking, daily, weekly and monthly reports, approvals and minutes, with Bluebeam, P6 and Procore preferred.

**Agent:** logs each RFI, submittal or field report, flags missing information, drafts the transmittal and periodic report, tracks ball-in-court dates, escalates overdue items; read-only in the pilot, the Office Engineer approves what goes out.

**Impact:** about 936–1,872 hours a year (6 posted roles x 2,080 hours x 15–25% on logs and reports x 50–60% automated; roles from your postings, rest our assumption) at $35.03/hour (bookkeeping and auditing clerks, NJ median $28.03, 2025, x1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $32,780–$65,570 a year.

**Pilot:** four weeks, one or two live projects.

## Why this works

- **People approve:** each draft waits for a human sign-off.
- **Your tools:** Excel, Outlook, Bluebeam, P6 and Procore; the site is WordPress.
- **Your data:** a private, client-scoped index, not a public chat tool.
