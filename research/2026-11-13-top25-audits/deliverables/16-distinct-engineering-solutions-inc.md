# Opportunity Audit — Distinct Engineering Solutions, Inc. (DESI)

Thirty-plus staff, seven service lines, an accredited lab and DBE/MBE certification in four states. Behind them sits one six-field contact form.

### 1. One front door, sorted by line

**Now:** no service picker, booking link or street address; different service lines arrive through it into one general inbox (checked November 2026).

**Agent would:** ask four to six scoping questions per line, check service area and prequalifications, reply with documents needed and offer a call with the right project manager.

**Impact:** about 50–100 staff hours a year (100–200 enquiries × 1 hour × 50% automated, our assumption) at $22.95/hour (NJ median, customer service reps, BLS May 2025; $28.69 with 1.25 benefits load, our assumption) = about $1,434–$2,869 a year.

**Pilot:** three weeks, three lines, first-reply time.

### 2. Inspection scheduling and report chasing

**Now:** your careers page plans 14-plus hires, including four NICET II/III construction inspectors, citing workload and backlog from existing contracts.

**Worth checking:** how much of a week goes on scheduling and chasing?

**Agent would:** draft a daily plan matching each job's certification needs to inspector credentials, send confirmations, flag clashes, then chase missing field data and late reports.

**Impact:** about 150–225 staff hours a year (250 working days × 2–3 hours a day × 30% automated, our assumption) at $22.95/hour (NJ median, customer service reps, BLS May 2025; $28.69 with 1.25 benefits load, our assumption) = about $4,303–$6,455 a year.

**Pilot:** four weeks on special inspection: certification matrix, report board.

### 3. Bids and proposal compliance

**Now:** a posted Proposal Coordinator role covers RFP requirements, deadlines and portal submissions, against prequalifications in three states and DBE/MBE in four.

**Agent would:** watch agency portals daily, post a short go/no-go brief, then build the compliance matrix, addendum calendar and draft firm-background sections.

**Impact:** about 144–240 staff hours a year (30–50 submissions × 12 hours × 40% automated, our assumption) at $22.95/hour (NJ median, customer service reps, BLS May 2025; $28.69 with 1.25 benefits load, our assumption) = about $4,131–$6,885 a year.

**Pilot:** four weeks, three portals, one live proposal.

### Why this works

- Runs on your website form, inboxes and calendars.
- People approve priced quotes, schedule changes and bid submissions; the agent drafts and routes.
- Documents stay in your accounts with an audit trail.

### Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid three-week pilot; the figures above use one conservative benchmark.
