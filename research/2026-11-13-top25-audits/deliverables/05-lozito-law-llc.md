## 1. Front door after 5pm

**Now:** As checked in November 2026: two pages, no form, no click-to-call link, no hours published on the site (a third-party listing shows 9–5), and titles reading Milltown against a North Brunswick address.

**Agent:** A contact page with a short intake form, tap-to-call links, your hours and the correct address, plus an assistant that acknowledges in seconds and hands both attorneys a one-page summary — no fees, no advice.

**Impact:** 1–3 after-hours enquiries a month × 1 in 3 converting (our assumptions) × $1,000–$2,500 NJ flat fee (sourced) = about $4,000–$30,000 a year in closing work.

**Pilot:** two weeks for the page, then the assistant.

## 2. Closing-file coordination

**Now:** No portal, upload, checklist or status page on the site; what runs the files, we could not see. Worth checking together: how files are tracked, and which calendar you use?

**Agent:** A per-file checklist that emails the party holding a missing item, chases on a cadence, sends milestone updates from approved templates and proposes signing slots from the calendar; anything at risk goes to an attorney.

**Impact:** about 90–540 staff hours a year (15–30 closings a month × 1–3 staff hours a file × 50% handed over, our assumptions) at $37/hour (legal-secretary median, NJ, 2025, ×1.25 for benefits) = about $3,330–$19,980 a year.

**Pilot:** three weeks on two live files with a time log.

## 3. Answers from your own files

**Now:** No FAQ, no checklist, no "what to bring to closing": the site answers none of the recurring closing questions.

**Agent:** Interview both attorneys once and draft the closing and estate-planning playbook for them to sign off; answers quote the passage used or say "not covered — ask the attorney".

**Impact:** about 65–173 staff hours a year (15–40 questions a week × 10 minutes × half answered in one pass, our assumptions) at $37/hour (legal-secretary median, NJ, 2025, ×1.25 for benefits) = about $2,400–$6,400 a year.

**Pilot:** two weeks of interviews to a signed playbook.

- Fits WordPress, Gmail and your calendar; work stays in firm-controlled accounts with retention off.
