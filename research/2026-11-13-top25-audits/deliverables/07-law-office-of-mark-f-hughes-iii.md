# Opportunity Audit — Mark F. Hughes III, Esquire

The online front door is one free Weebly page from 2015, offering DWI and refusal work from rented suites in two towns. Its "CALL NOW" button does nothing when tapped.

## 1. A front door that works on a phone

**Now** (checked November 2026): no form, email, hours or booking, and no structured data for search assistants; one page, last modified 21 May 2015. Worth checking together: where do after-hours calls land?

**The agent would:** build four to six pages on your own domain, naming the courts you appear in, with hours, tap-to-call and one form to one inbox.

**Impact:** about 104–208 staff hours a year (52 weeks x 2–4 hours x all of that typing automated, our assumption) at $37.91/hour (legal-secretary median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $3,942–$7,885 a year.

**Pilot:** two to four weeks, live, with a baseline.

## 2. A deadline and court-date clerk

**Now:** the attorney's name and ID (021041994) appear on New Jersey's administrative ineligibility lists repeatedly since 2018, including 2024, 2025 and the list ending 9 November 2026. Compliance lists, not discipline findings; a listed attorney is not allowed to practise.

**The agent would:** keep one register of dated obligations with 60/30/7-day reminders, pre-fill the paperwork for your signature, watch the published lists, and merge forwarded court notices into one calendar.

**Impact:** about 16–40 staff hours a year (40 dated items x 0.4–1.0 hours x all of that work automated, our assumption) at $37.91/hour (legal-secretary median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $606–$1,516 a year.

**Pilot:** two to four weeks, starting with remediation.

## 3. An after-hours intake line

**Now:** the site publishes no hours, form, booking or after-hours option.

**The agent would:** answer after-hours texts and web enquiries, take charge, county and court date, reply from your written FAQ, offer two slots and escalate custody or same-day matters.

**Impact:** about 60–180 attorney hours a year (12 months x 5–15 hours x all of that time handled, our assumption) at $97.01/hour (lawyer median, NJ, 2025, x1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $5,820–$17,461 a year.

**Pilot:** two to four weeks, text and web, logged.

## Why this works
- Anything filed, quoted or sent waits for your sign-off.
- It fits a phone, an inbox and a calendar.
- Caller data stays on zero-retention endpoints.

## Suggested first step
A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot on the deadline register.
