# Opportunity Audit — Premier Properties of New Brunswick

Roughly 350 units (a 2022 NJBIZ figure) across a dozen downtown buildings, 25 years in one city, 2026–27 availability pre-leasing now. The front door is a phone call: "CALL FOR DETAILS" heads the price list, and on a phone the contact page runs off-screen.

## 1. Answer and book, not just ring

**Now (checked November 2026):** a dozen buildings at $1,210–$4,200, some marked unavailable; a form with no phone field; at 390 pixels the page lays out 768 wide.

**Would do:** phone-first pages with an availability-and-tour form taking phone, unit type and move-in date; prices, hours and address published for search engines and AI assistants to quote.

**Impact:** bookable on a phone, not a Monday call-back. Each extra lease is $14,520–$50,400 gross a year (12 × your rents; the $1,210 studio is unavailable, so the floor is higher). A marketplace test saw up to +4.8% scheduled visits (preprint).

**Pilot:** 2–3 weeks, five pages.

## 2. After-hours desk

**Now:** hours are published as Monday–Friday 9:00 AM–5:00 PM, no weekend hours listed; your $22/hour Leasing Assistant posting bundled calls, applications, tours and deposits.

**Would do:** text back missed calls and web enquiries, ask unit type, move-in date and a callable number, answer from your FAQ, log it in AppFolio.

**Impact:** about 42–186 staff hours a year (350 units × 2–4 enquiries × 12–20 minutes × 30–40% automated, our assumptions) at $27.50/hour (your $22 posting × 1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $1,155–$5,115 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, one cluster.

## 3. One price source of truth

**Now:** AppFolio is your system of record (footer badge, portal — our read of your site), yet availability is hand-typed on a © 2022 page, and the Preferred Tenants List takes an application, a W-9, an agreement and a $500 refundable deposit, with no online path published.

**Would do:** a nightly read-only AppFolio pull into one feed driving the site; an online start where a document agent flags missing items and drafts deposit steps for your approval.

**Impact:** about 10–84 staff hours a year (80–240 applications × 15–30 minutes × 50–70% automated, our assumptions) at $27.50/hour ($22 × 1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $275–$2,310 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–4 weeks, ten packets.

## Why this works

- Runs evenings and weekends; deposits go to a person.
- Plugs into your site (it looks like a Duda build), AppFolio and your Google listing; files stay in your screening flow.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot on one cluster.
