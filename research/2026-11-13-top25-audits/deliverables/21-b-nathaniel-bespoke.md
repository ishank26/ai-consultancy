# Opportunity Audit — B. Nathaniel Bespoke

You are a custom clothier and alterations shop in New Brunswick, selling suits, wedding packages and alterations, with 4.8 from 497 reviews. The tailoring is well reviewed; the way in is a free-text form or a phone call.

## 1. One front door, one set of facts

**Now:** "Book Now" opens a free-text form with no calendar, slots or prices, and no confirmation step visible (checked 13 Nov 2026); weekday opening shows as 9:00am, 9:30am and 10:00am in three places.

**Agent:** keeps one approved record of phone, hours and services, adds a scheduler that confirms in writing, and flags differences across sites and listings weekly.

**Impact:** about 156–312 staff hours a year (780–1,560 inquiries a year x 20 minutes x 60% handled — our assumption) at $25.03/hour ($20.02 NJ receptionist median, 2025, x1.25 for benefits — our assumption) = about $3,904–$7,809 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–3 weeks; scheduler live.

## 2. Fittings booked without phone tag

**Now:** the site says staff call you to arrange the first fitting, home and office visits add travel, and the published email is a Gmail address (bnatbespoke@gmail.com).

**Agent:** watches the form, missed calls and inbox, books slots, reminds and re-offers; anything outside your bands goes to a person.

**Impact:** about 260–624 staff hours a year (1,040–1,664 appointments a year x 30–45 minutes x 50% handled — our assumption) at $25.03/hour (same wage, plus the 25% benefits load — our assumption) = about $6,507–$15,618 a year.

**Pilot:** 3–4 weeks, one service line.

## 3. Answering "how much and how soon"

**Now:** the site lists no prices and the alterations page no rates; starting prices appear on the older site ($495 suits, $595 tuxedos); the FAQ carries general answers but no prices or turnaround.

**Agent:** replies with your signed-off price band and ready date after two questions, hands off rush, gown and bulk work, and extends the FAQ with those bands.

**Impact:** about 104–260 staff hours a year (624–1,040 price questions a year x 20 minutes x 50–75% handled — our assumption) at $25.94/hour ($20.75 NJ tailor and dressmaker median, 2025, x1.25 for benefits — our assumption) = about $2,697–$6,744 a year.

**Pilot:** 2–3 weeks: bands for your busiest services.

These pockets partly overlap — don't add them together.

## Open issues from the checker

- Section 3: "the site lists no prices ... starting prices appear on the older site ($495 suits, $595 tuxedos)" and founder email: "your live site lists no starting prices while the older one shows $495 suits".: Contradicted. The current site's own shop page (https://www.bnathanielbespoke.com/shop-mens-suits/Mens-Suits-c139701855) contains six product cards (Men's Suit, Wedding Suit) each priced $495.00, and in one browser session, once the storefront script had hydrated, those prices were visibly rendered on the page (6/6 price elements with layout, body text containing '$495.00'). On cold loads the store grid sits hidden until the Ecwid store script runs, which is probably why the researcher saw no prices, but the price is in the live page and can appear to a visitor. So the audit's central contrast - prices survive only on the abandoned site - is wrong, and the $495 figure in the email is attributed to the wrong site.
- Section 2 impact total: "at $25.03/hour ... = about $6,507-$15,618 a year".: Arithmetic slip. $20.02 x 1.25 = $25.025; 624 hours x $25.025 = $15,615.60, i.e. about $15,616, not $15,618. The low end ($6,507) and Section 1 and 3 totals recompute correctly.
- Volume inputs in all three impact lines: "780-1,560 inquiries a year", "1,040-1,664 appointments a year", "624-1,040 price questions a year".: These volumes are invented for this audit, but only the minutes and handled-share carry "our assumption" and no basis is shown, so a reader cannot see where 15-30 inquiries or 20-32 appointments a week comes from. Ranges should show their assumptions.
- Section 2 "Now": "home and office visits add travel".: Presented as an observed fact about the page. The site only states that tailors come to the customer; that this creates travel coordination is an inference.
- Intro and email: "4.8 from 497 reviews".: Verified only on a claimed Birdeye profile that mirrors Google review data; Google's own listing could not be opened. The audit states it as a bare fact of the business and the email does not say where the number comes from.
- "Why this works": "It works with your tools - the form, Gmail, your calendar.": The form and Gmail address are sourced; a calendar is assumed - nothing on the site shows which calendar the shop uses.
