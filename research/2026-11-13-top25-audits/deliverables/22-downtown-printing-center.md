# Opportunity Audit — Downtown Printing Center

Downtown Printing Center has printed in downtown New Brunswick since 1977 and sits at 46 Paterson St.: digital, offset, apparel and counter services. Its site is a four-page © 2022 GoDaddy build; the home page carries only a logo, one stock photo and the footer, with no address, hours or services as text, and no prices appear anywhere on the site.

## 1. Fix the front door

**Now (checked Nov 2026):** a logo, a stock photo, a © 2022 footer; the intake form is Name/Email/Message with no size, quantity or artwork, and hours differ across listings (9:00–6:00 vs 8:30–5:30).

**The agent:** product pages you approve, a quote form with size, quantity, stock, deadline and artwork, and review replies drafted for you.

**Impact:** about 40–150 staff hours a year (50 weeks x 1–3 hours a week x 80–100% automated; our assumption) at $28.69/hour (O*NET Customer Service Rep median, NJ, BLS 2025: $47,740 ÷ 2,080 x1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $1,147–$4,303 a year.

**Pilot:** two to three weeks — three product pages, live quote form, hours fixed.

## 2. Add a quote desk

**Now:** quotes arrive by phone or a free-text message with no artwork upload; the counter also runs money transfers and passport photos. Worth checking together: how much of a day goes on chasing specs?

**The agent:** reads each request, fills in size, quantity, stock, deadline and artwork, drafts a price from past jobs; you approve prices.

**Impact:** about 37–160 staff hours a year (50 weeks x 3–8 hours a week x 25–40% automated; our assumption) at $28.69/hour = about $1,061–$4,590 a year.

**Pilot:** three to four weeks in shadow mode on 30–50 past jobs.

## 3. Answer questions on tap

**Now:** no prices, samples or turnaround are published on the site; a 2016 consent order ended its authorization to print state prescription blanks after verification failures.

**The agent:** reads your price lists and past quotes, answers with the source, and says when something isn't in the documents.

**Impact:** about 16–33 staff hours a year (20–40 questions a week x 2 minutes each x 50% handled, over 50 weeks; our assumption) at $28.69/hour = about $459–$946 a year.

**Pilot:** three weeks over 100–300 pages, tested on 40 counter questions.

## Why this works

- **Autonomy with limits and your approval:** they draft and follow up, but prices, pages and review replies wait for your sign-off.
- **Your data:** documents stay in your hands.

**Suggested first step:** a 20-minute walkthrough at the counter, then a small paid pilot.

Prepared from publicly available information. Estimates are illustrative and would be confirmed in a discovery session.

## For the founder (not part of the audit)

**Email draft:** Your site is friendly to look at but hard to use — the home page carries no address or hours as text, the Services page is a one-screen bullet list with no stock, sizes, turnaround or price, and the form asks for a message rather than a quantity or a file. A customer can't see a price or send artwork without calling. If it's useful, I would take twenty minutes at the counter to show you what a rebuilt front door and an automatic quote assistant would look like.

**Walk-in opener:** We build software that turns quote requests into priced, spec-complete jobs — could I show you one thing on your site a customer can't do today?
