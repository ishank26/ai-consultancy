# Opportunity Audit — Deborah A. Rose, P.C.

You are on the November 13, 2026 Middlesex mediator roster at a published $300 an hour, and your Avvo and Lawyers.com profiles carry strong client reviews. What a client sees first is different: rosedeb.com no longer loads.

## 1. Put the front door back

**What's happening now (checked 13 November 2026):** rosedeb.com fails to resolve though paid to June 2027; the unclaimed Google profile shows no hours and links there; darosepc.com is a parked page.

**What the agent would do:** restore the domain, publish five pages with your roster rate and towns served, and claim and correct the listing.

**Pilot:** two weeks.

## 2. Bilingual intake that answers when you can't

**What's happening now:** the roster lists a phone, fax and email, but nothing on the web is under your control; online enquiries arrive through a directory form that captures the lead first. Worth checking together: your weekly enquiry count.

**What the agent would do:** answer calls and web enquiries in English and Spanish, note both parties' names for your conflict check, offer three calendar slots, and escalate urgent matters.

**Pilot:** three weeks, scored against 30 real enquiries.

## 3. Drafts and updates from your own files

**What's happening now:** your archived site says the firm is kept small for one-on-one attention, and reviewers praise being kept informed; no portal or upload page appears.

**What the agent would do:** a private, per-matter assistant over your briefs, letters and published decisions would answer with quoted passages and file references, check citations, and queue drafts for your approval.

**Pilot:** four weeks, four files, 50 scored questions.

## Why this works

- It sits behind your phone, calendar and email.
- You approve each new matter, fee quote and message; steps are logged.
- Client files stay in a private setup that trains on nothing.

A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid two-week pilot.
