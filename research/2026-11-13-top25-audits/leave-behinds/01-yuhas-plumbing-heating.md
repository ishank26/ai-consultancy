# Opportunity Audit — Yuhas Plumbing & Heating

Thirty-five-plus years in business at 33 Jackson St, licensed plumbing #7275 and HVAC #147600, a Carrier Factory Authorized Dealer. The front door is thinner: a phone line, a Gmail address, buttons that don't connect.

## 1. Three call buttons, two wrong numbers

**Today** (checked 13 Nov 2026): Google Maps reads "Claim this business", no hours or website link. Two of the three call buttons dial wrong numbers (nine digits, and transposed digits); /contact returns 404.

**The agent would** claim the Google and Nextdoor profiles with hours and a site link, fix each number to tap-to-call, add a quote form routing urgent jobs to (908) 482-8296, and request post-job reviews.

**Pilot** profile claimed, links fixed, form live in weeks 1–2.

## 2. A line that answers when the crew can't

**Today** hours sit on third-party listings (Monday–Friday 9–6, weekends closed), with no published after-hours route for the emergency number.

**The agent would** answer missed and after-hours calls, ask set questions, alert the on-call tech, book routine work into windows, and text a confirmation. The owner approves each emergency dispatch.

**Pilot** one number forwarded after hours; a person reviews the transcripts.

## 3. Estimates and invoices that stop sitting

**Today** no scheduling, invoicing or customer-record tool appears publicly; contact is by Gmail. One review describes a reasonable estimate cancelled after a question.

**The agent would** take job photos and a voice note, draft the estimate for owner approval, follow up on days 2 and 7, then draft the invoice.

**Pilot** one job type end to end for a month.

## Why this works

- A person approves each price and dispatch.
- It works with your numbers and inbox.
- Recording is off by default; transcripts follow a written policy.

Worth checking together: how after-hours calls are handled now, and how many estimates go quiet? A 20-minute walkthrough at 33 Jackson St, then a small paid pilot.
