# Opportunity Audit — Federation Logistics, LLC

Federation Logistics runs food-grade, bonded and liquor-licensed warehousing and port drayage from North Brunswick, with four New Jersey sites and 500,000-plus square feet claimed. On fedlogistics.com the top navigation does not open (checked November 2026).

### 1. A phone-friendly front door

**What's happening now** (checked November 2026): About us, Services and Contact point to "#", so no service pages exist; published hours disagree; below 1439px the call button is hidden.

**What the agent would do** One page per service, working navigation, one hours block, tap-to-call, an assistant answering from approved copy.

**Pilot** Two to three weeks.

### 2. Quotes answered, then chased

**What's happening now** The one online quote route is a ~70-field "Customer Survey" form that posts back to quote.php with no confirmation screen, no reference number and no auto-reply visible on the site.

**What the agent would do** Acknowledge at once with a reference, turn free text into a fixed request sheet, ask what's missing, chase at 2/5/10 days; a person approves each rate.

**Pilot** Three to four weeks.

### 3. Bond and licence paperwork answered

**What's happening now** The site lists FDA, UIIA, bonded, SQF and ABC scope but publishes no upload or document-request route. Worth checking together: how do your importers' CBP deadlines (entry documents within 15 calendar days of arrival, entry summary within 10 working days) reach your team?

**What the agent would do** Answer from your own procedures with citations, draft missing-document requests, build a per-shipment checklist, run deadline clocks as fixed rules; your compliance owner approves each filing.

**Pilot** Four weeks, one scope.

**Why this works**
- A person approves every rate, answer and filing.
- It sits on your quote form, mailbox and calendar.
- Client files stay private, with a log of what was sent.

**Suggested first step** A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot.
