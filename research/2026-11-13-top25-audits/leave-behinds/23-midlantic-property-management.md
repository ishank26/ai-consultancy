# Opportunity Audit — Midlantic Property Management

Midlantic has managed New Jersey community associations since 1995 from Highland Park and Long Branch, with a Little Egg Harbor field office. A board finds a Weebly site with a 2013 footer, no working contact form, and a GoDaddy parking page at mpm-nj.com (checked 13 Nov 2026).

**1. A front door that works after 4:30 p.m.**
*Now:* proposals arrive by phone or a hand-typed email; the work-order page offers a Submit button and nothing to fill in. Hours end 4:30; the Google profile is unclaimed, 1.6 stars, no hours.
*The agent:* proposal and resident request forms capturing those fields, a claimed profile with hours, a readable site on the owned domain.

*Pilot:* 2–3 weeks.

**2. Work-order triage with a receipt for the resident**
*Now:* residents phone (732) 828-7200 or email a free-text template; the firm says after-hours calls reach a live operator who takes the facts and arranges dispatch.
*The agent:* reads each request, applies written emergency criteria, opens a ticket, acknowledges the resident, routes routine work to the manager; exceptions go to a person.

*Pilot:* 3–4 weeks on two associations.

**3. Delinquency letters that end with a human signature**
*Now:* the FAQ sets out monthly assessments, a 30-day delinquency stage, late charges, lien notices and foreclosure; the firm advertises AP, AR, accountant and administrator roles.
*The agent:* recomputes balances, applies each association's collection policy, drafts the stage letter, stops while a dispute is open; corrections and lien referrals wait for a named person.

*Pilot:* 3–4 weeks replaying one association's payments.

## Why this works
- Nothing leaves on its own: proposals, prices and lien referrals wait for a named sign-off.
- It sits in front of your phone, email and accounting system.
- Resident records stay staff-only and logged.

**Suggested first step:** a 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot on one association.
