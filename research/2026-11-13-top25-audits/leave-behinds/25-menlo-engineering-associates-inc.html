Opportunity Audit — Menlo Engineering Associates, Inc.

Sixty years, 3,000-plus projects, a 281,215-square-foot Parsippany facility announced in June 2026 — yet in November 2026 the home page has no headline and the Projects and News pages print page-builder code.

1. A front door that works

Now: as of November 2026 both index pages print raw page-builder code instead of projects or news, and the home page has no headline, no button above the fold and no tel: link.

Agent: a home page with headline, six services, address and one "start a project" button; repaired indexes; intake capturing municipality, lot, timeline and files, plus a one-page brief for the project manager.

Pilot: 3 weeks; before/after counts of inquiries and replies.

2. Earlier notice of new applications

Now: where do new opportunities come from today? In November 2025 a firm vice president was sworn in as an applicant's expert at Riverdale's Planning Board, and a $120K–$135K project-manager role is open.

Agent: daily reading of the NJ legal-notices directory and 10–15 towns' agendas into a weekly go/no-go brief, proposal drafts and a hearing calendar; a principal approves each.

Pilot: 3–4 weeks; one brief and two proposal drafts.

3. Review-letter judgement kept in house

Now: the designer posting asks for NJDEP stormwater rules, the firm's announcement says two long-serving staff have moved to part-time advisory roles ahead of retirement, and review letters need point-by-point answers under N.J.A.C. 7:8. Where do past letters sit?

Agent: a private, access-controlled index of your letters, the rule text and town checklists that drafts a response per comment with citation and closest precedent; a P.E. reviews and seals each draft.

Pilot: 2–4 weeks on one live project, with an accuracy log.

Why this works

  • A person approves at each gate — manager, principal, P.E.
  • Runs on WordPress, Gravity Forms and email; client files stay access-controlled, which matters with retailers and municipalities on the client list.

Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot.
