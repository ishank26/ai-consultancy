Opportunity Audit — Bussel Realty Corp

BRC has marketed industrial and office space across Central and North Jersey since 1984 and says it handles three million square feet a year. Your New Brunswick page lists the Edison suite.

1. The front door today

What a customer sees (checked 13 November 2026): a 23-broker directory with a plain-text main number, no enquiry form, no booking and no tap-to-call. bussel.com/contact-us/ returns a raw IIS error page. Open listings #27516 and #23253 print "[-address on file-]"; exclusive #26961 shows a street address. Search results load only in a browser.

What the agent would do: with a developer, fix the orphan /contact-us/ path, give the working contact page an enquiry path, make numbers tappable, and make listings readable to search engines.

Pilot: 2–4 weeks, ten live listings readable.

2. Enquiry intake and routing

The contact page offers 23 broker mobiles and one admin extension; the one structured form is seller-side (property valuation). Worth checking together: how enquiries arrive, how many come after hours, and who replies first?

What the agent would do: capture type, size, location, budget and timing, route each enquiry to the best-fit broker, follow up at 24 and 72 hours; brokers confirm tours and address releases.

Pilot: one county, two weeks, against a call baseline.

3. Collateral and market reports

You are hiring a full-time marketing/admin assistant at $42,000–$50,000 for brochures, flyers, research and press, while your Quarterly Report news section shows nothing.

What the agent would do: draft flyer, web summary, social post, deal release and quarterly snapshot from one listing record, for approval.

Pilot: 8–10 listings and one Middlesex snapshot.

Why this works

  • A person approves every address release and pack.
  • It runs on the existing listing database and CRM login.
  • Enquiries are logged and reviewable.

Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough of these findings, then a small paid pilot.
