Opportunity Audit — Hollander Electric Co LLC

Thirty-eight years trading, three master licences and an NJ home-improvement registration, commercial and industrial work as the stated focus. What caught our eye: 30 town pages with no page titles.

1. Titles and a booking path for 30 town pages

Now (checked 13 November 2026): all 31 pages have an empty title, no description and no structured data; the sitemap lists the homepage alone.

Build: per-town titles and descriptions from existing copy, structured data with the three master licences and the registration number, redirects for old addresses, and a five-field form with call windows.

Pilot: two weeks, validated in Search Console.

2. An after-hours line that answers

Now: 24/7 emergency response is advertised to commercial clients, but the web form is same-business-day and published hours (Birdeye) are Mon–Thu 8:00–4:30, Fri 8:00–4:00, closed weekends.

Build: a voice and text agent answering after hours and on overflow — what is down, panel size, access — flagging commercial emergencies to the on-call electrician, who accepts before overtime.

Pilot: four weeks on one number, weekly transcript review.

3. Watch the panels you already service

Now: public pages show switchgear, motor controls, standby generators and an industrial maintenance programme; nothing public shows what happens between visits. Worth checking together: is a visit the first look?

Build: clamp-on current and temperature sensors at two or three sites plus generator exercise signals; drift raises a draft ticket to a named person. Readings stay on site; no camera, no audio, no face recognition; a person reviews alerts.

Pilot: four weeks at one maintenance customer, 30–60 days of baseline.

Why this works

  • Nothing is dispatched, priced or promised without human approval.
  • It sits beside the website, Google profile and reviews already in use.
  • Readings and customer details stay with you; alerts are advisory.

Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid two-week pilot, priced before we start.
