# Opportunity Audit — Adway Services Inc

You run a small IT staffing firm in East Brunswick (public records put headcount around 14). On 13 November 2026 every page we opened on adwaytech.com, the site your Google listing links to, returned a Cloudflare error.

## 1. Reachable front door

**Now:** On 13 November 2026 no page on adwaytech.com returned readable content, yet your Google listing still points to it.

**Agent:** A live site with tappable phone and email, pages per service, a text summary assistants can read, and two intake forms.

**Pilot:** 2–3 weeks to a live site and two forms.

## 2. Résumé sorting

**Now:** Your last saved careers page shows an "Open Job Requirements" heading with the jobs switched off in the HTML, and sends résumés by post to the HR Dept.; worth checking whether any also arrive by email.

**Agent:** It reads each résumé, pulls skills, years, work authorisation and location, and ranks candidates against open roles. A recruiter approves any candidate before a client sees it.

**Pilot:** 3 weeks on one role family, against your own baseline.

## 3. Compliance deadlines

**Now:** Public records show 81 H-1B labor condition applications (LCAs), 9 prevailing-wage requests and 5 PERM filings, placements in eight states, newest wage request 11 June 2026; a second tracker lists 34, so counts differ by source and years.

**Agent:** It drafts posting notices and wage memos from your filings, keeps a dated ledger per consultant (posting window, access file, expiry) and flags what falls due in 30, 60 or 90 days. Counsel signs each filing; it never files with DOL.

**Pilot:** 3 weeks: 12 months of filings, one ledger, two draft notices.

## Why this works

- A named person approves what reaches a client, and each filing.
- It runs on your existing email, domain and Google listing.
- Immigration documents stay in your storage, not a public model.
