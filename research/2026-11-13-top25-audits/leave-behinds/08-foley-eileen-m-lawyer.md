# Opportunity Audit — The Law Office of Eileen M. Foley, Esq.

A family-law practice since 1990, run by an attorney admitted in 1989. Referrals bring most clients, so we checked what a referral sees.

## 1. Your front door

**What's happening now** (checked 13 November 2026): the secure site returns a certificate warning, the contact page has no enquiry form or tap-to-call link, and Google advertises "Online appointments" with nothing to book.

**What the agent would do**: fix the certificate and force https, add tap-to-call and a consultation form, make address, hours and practice areas machine-readable, and correct the Avvo and Lawyers.com listings.

**Pilot**: two weeks, tested on a phone.

## 2. When you are in court

**What's happening now**: enquiries come by phone (Monday–Friday 9–5) or email; the site offers no form, scheduler or after-hours route, and a free 15-minute phone consultation comes first.

**What the agent would do**: take details from a form, email or missed call, pre-check the other party against your matter list, and book only with your approval.

**Pilot**: three weeks, form and email first.

## 3. Paperwork that starts itself

**What's happening now**: no client portal, upload, e-signature or payment link is published on the site; documents go by email or fax.

**Worth checking together**: how are engagement letters and document checklists assembled today?

**What the agent would do**: draft the engagement letter from your template, raise the retainer invoice at your amount, request e-signature, and serve a checklist on a secure upload page.

**Pilot**: three weeks on divorce files.

## Why this works

- It drafts and routes on tools a practice already has; you approve each booking, document and listing change.
- Client details stay in an account you control; no legal advice, no fee decisions.

## Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough (including after-hours enquiry volume), then a small paid two-week pilot.
