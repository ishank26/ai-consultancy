Website Health Check: Hua Kang Health Center

What we saw on 13 November 2026

  • huakangskincare.com, the shop's own site since 2014, now redirects to huakangskincare.com/lander, a GoDaddy parking page reading "parked free, courtesy of GoDaddy.com", with a "Get This Domain" button and unrelated skin-care search topics (screenshots saved).
  • Google's profile for Hua Kang Health Center (118 Ryders Ln, Milltown NJ 08850; (732) 253-7885; 4.4 stars; "Massage therapist") shows no website - only an "Add website" prompt.
  • Archives show facials, skin care and hot-stone massage pages; the last working capture is 29 February 2024, and by 3 April 2024 the address served the parking redirect.
  • Two older domains linked from that site are unusable: huakanghealthcenter.com does not resolve; huakangspa.com returns an HTTP 522 error.

What it means for customers

Anyone finding the shop on Google gets hours, a phone number and reviews, but nothing about treatments or prices. Anyone typing the old address lands on a page offering the domain for sale.

How to fix it

  1. Check the registrar account holding huakangskincare.com and who controls it, and switch on auto-renew - owner or office manager, 1-2 hours.
  2. If it is yours, rebuild a one-page site on it (services, hours, tap-to-call) - web helper, 4-8 hours.
  3. If it is not yours, register a new domain, publish the page there and stop printing the old address - owner plus helper, 3-6 hours.
  4. Put the working address in the Google profile's empty website field and correct directories carrying the old domain - owner or helper, 1-2 hours.
  5. Diarise a yearly check of the domain - half an hour.

After the fix

Add a tap-to-call button and a short appointment-request form, and link the page from the Google profile, which shows no way to book online today.
