Website Health Check: E and J Electrical Contractor LLC

What we saw on 13 November 2026

  • eandjelectrical.com, the address on the company's CIS trade listing, redirects to a GoDaddy page reading "eandjelectrical.com is parked free, courtesy of GoDaddy.com", with a "Get This Domain" button and unrelated search links (screenshots attached).
  • The domain is still registered - created 9 Dec 2008, expires 9 Dec 2027, registrar GoDaddy (public RDAP) - so it has not lapsed and no one else holds it.
  • The Internet Archive shows the real E&J site - Services, Our Company, six named projects, Contact - on 24 Jul 2024; by 21 Dec 2024 the same address was already the parked page.
  • Google's listing "e and j electrical" (501 Adams Ln, (732) 658-3560, 5.0 rating, category "Utility contractor") is unclaimed and has no website; CIS Leads still offers a "Website" button to the parked domain.

What it means for customers

A buyer who looks the firm up lands on a page inviting offers for the domain, and Google offers them no site at all. Services, project history and contact then depend on a phone call.

How to fix it

  1. Find who can log in to the GoDaddy account, and switch on auto-renew (owner, 0.5-1 hr).
  2. Choose: rebuild a small site from the archived pages (developer, 6-12 hrs), or redirect the domain to the live site (1-3 hrs).
  3. Claim the Google listing and attach the site, hours and correct category (owner, 1-2 hrs).
  4. Fix the CIS "Website" button and the stale address/phone variants on other directories (office manager, 2-4 hrs).
  5. Confirm email on the domain still works, then set renewal and uptime alerts (IT, 1-2 hrs).

After the fix

The Google listing is unclaimed, so no one can add a website, hours or answer reviews. Claiming it changes what a searching buyer sees first.
