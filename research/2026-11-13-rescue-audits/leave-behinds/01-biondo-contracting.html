What it means for customers

A homeowner who taps the website on your profile lands on a gambling page and finds no services, towns served or quote form. The phone number is still listed on your profile, AECinfo and Birdeye, so enquiries depend on a call.

How to fix it

  1. Confirm who holds the GoDaddy account and turn on two-step verification (owner and site builder, 0.5-1 hour).

  3. Publish a simple one-page site: services, towns, phone, licence number, photos, contact form (developer, 2-4 hours).
  4. Update the website field on Google plus the AECinfo, FindTrustedRoofers and Webflow links (owner, 1-2 hours).
  5. Block the gambling URL in Search Console's Removals tool, refresh stale results, add monitoring (developer, 1-2 hours).
