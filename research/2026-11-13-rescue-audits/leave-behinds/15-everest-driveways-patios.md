# Website Health Check: Everest Driveways & Patios

**What we saw on 13 November 2026**

- everestdriveways.com — the website on the Google profile and the Birdeye listing — returns `DNS_PROBE_FINISHED_NXDOMAIN` in Chrome at desktop and phone width; the .com registry holds no RDAP record for the name.
- Google Maps: "Everest Driveways & Patios", masonry contractor, 4.4 stars, 4400 US-9 #1000, Freehold NJ, (732) 688-7413, "Claim this business", website everestdriveways.com.
- Birdeye lists the same brand at 153 Joyce Kilmer Ave, New Brunswick, (732) 784-2976, 5.0 from 395 reviews, also linking www.everestdriveways.com. Its last archived capture, 14 February 2026, was a stub page redirecting to `/lander`.

**What it means for customers**

Anyone tapping the website link on Google or Birdeye gets a browser error, so no services, photos or quote form are reachable. Both listings still display a phone number, so calls can get through.

**How to fix it**

1. Check the registrar account for everestdriveways.com — renewable, in redemption, or released? (owner, 0.5-1 h)
2. If it is gone, register a new domain with simple hosting. (owner + web helper, 1-3 h)
3. Publish a small 4-5 page site: services, service area, photos, licence details, tap-to-call and a photo quote form. (web helper, 8-20 h)
4. Claim the Google profile, replace the website link, confirm the current address and phone. (owner, 1-2 h)
5. Correct the dead link on Birdeye and other directories; ask for stale duplicates to be removed. (owner or helper, 2-4 h)

**After the fix**

Claim and consolidate the Google profile: it carries "Claim this business" today, and the two listings disagree on address and phone. A claimed profile can hold the new link, photos and services.
