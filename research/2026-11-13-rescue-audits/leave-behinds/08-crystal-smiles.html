Website Health Check: Crystal Smiles (formerly No Tears Dental)

What we saw on 13 November 2026

  • The practice is open: Google shows Crystal Smiles LLC, 65 Rues Ln, East Brunswick, (732) 257-4444, rated 4.8, with no website - only an "Add website" prompt.
  • crystalsmilesnj.com, listed as its website by BizArchive, redirects to the domain reseller HugeDomains, behind a Cloudflare bot check.
  • notearsdental.com, still linked from the practice's Nextdoor page, shows a GoDaddy "parked free" page with a "Get This Domain" button.
  • Archived copies show the site at crystalsmilesnj.com on 7 June 2023, and a GoDaddy parking page there by 31 October 2023; no working site of its own was found.

What it means for customers

A patient who follows the website link on Nextdoor or a directory reaches a parking or reseller page, so hours, services and accepted insurance cannot be checked before calling. Everything depends on the phone.

How to fix it

  1. Confirm who holds each domain account and whether either can be renewed or bought back (1-3 hours, owner).
  2. Pick one domain, or register a new name, and stop linking to a dead one (1-3 hours).
  3. Add the site link to the Google profile and correct the Nextdoor and BizArchive entries (2-4 hours, office manager).
  4. Rebuild a small site: services, hours, accepted insurance (and whether Medicaid or Medicare Advantage plans are taken), map, tap-to-call, appointment request (8-20 hours, web person).
  5. Turn on auto-renew, registrar two-factor login and an expiry alert (0.5-2 hours).

After the fix

Add an online appointment request. The Google profile offered no booking action, only a phone number.
