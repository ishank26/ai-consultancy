# Website Health Check: WebTheory Media (WebTheory PPC)

## What we saw on 13 November 2026
- **webtheoryppc.com** loads but shows an Indonesian lottery site ("OLI4D ... Toto Macau") with a log-in and sign-up pop-up; no agency name or contact appears in the visible text, and an old agency page (/google-ads-audit/) now returns 404.
- Two "WebTheory PPC" Google listings, both rated 5.0, send visitors there from their "Website" buttons (14 Old Bridge Tpke Ste 203, (732) 655-9038; 607 Lake Ave, (732) 800-9867).
- Archives show the agency's Google Ads blog on the domain on 19 May 2026; the domain record shows registration since 2019 and expiry in 2027, so it did not lapse.
- The older domain webtheorymedia.com, which used to reach this site, now serves a Namecheap parking page that our browser did not render (blank page).

## What it means for customers
A customer tapping "Website" on Google lands on gambling content, so they cannot read the services or ask for a quote. The business still looks open: both listings show 5.0 and business hours.

## How to fix it
1. Secure the domain accounts: new passwords, two-step sign-in, and a check of recent domain settings and contact changes (owner, 1-3 hours).
2. Decide whether to rebuild on this domain or move to a new one (owner, 2-6 hours).
3. Repoint the "Website" button on both listings and resolve the duplicate listing (owner, 0.5-1.5 hours).
4. Rebuild a five-page site with services, proof and a contact form, then update directory listings (developer and copywriter, 10-25 hours).
5. Ask Google to drop the spam URLs and set uptime, domain and expiry alerts (developer, 1-3 hours).

## After the fix
The two Google listings, with their 5.0 ratings and hours, appear to be the main way customers find the business today; once they point at a working site, adding tap-to-call would let a visitor act in one tap.
