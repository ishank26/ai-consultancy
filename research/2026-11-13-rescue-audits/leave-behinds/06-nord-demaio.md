# Website Health Check: Nord & DeMaio (now trading as NordLaw)

**What we saw on 13 November 2026**

- The firm's original domain, nord-demaio.com, refuses HTTPS connections; plain HTTP serves a Chinese-language dehumidifier company page whose title and description promote an online gambling brand.
- That domain's registration record now starts on 27 March 2025 (registrar Gname.com Pte. Ltd.), about six years after the firm's last archived use (26 January 2019).
- The current site, nordlaw.legal, is live, and the Google profile (NordLaw, Metroplex, 190 NJ-18 Ste 201, (732) 214-0303) links to it.
- One directory still points to the old domain: Lawyers.com lists http://www.nord-demaio.com under Additional Links. The current site also lays out at 980 px wide on a 390 px phone, so it appears shrunk instead of fitted.

**What it means for customers**

A client who arrives through that directory profile sees dehumidifier pages and gambling text instead of the firm's site. They may also be unsure which site is genuinely the firm's.

**How to fix it**

1. Find out who holds the nord-demaio.com registrar account and whether renewal was missed (0.5-1 hour).
2. If it is not recoverable, remove it from Additional Links on Lawyers.com, email signatures, letterhead and cards (1-2 hours).
3. Confirm the Google profile still points at nordlaw.legal (0.5 hour).
4. Protect nordlaw.legal: auto-renew, 2FA, and a reminder before the 9 January 2027 expiry (1 hour).
5. Rebuild the site with a true mobile layout, one page per practice area (20-40 hours).

**After the fix**

The NordLaw Google profile has 2 reviews at 3.0 while the site cites nearly 40 years of practice. Asking for a review at the end of each matter is the simplest next gain.
