# Website Health Check: Advance Home Renovations LLC

**What we saw on 13 November 2026**

- The Google profile (5.0, "General contractor", New Brunswick, NJ, (973) 905-2129) lists advancehomereno.com as its website. The domain does not resolve in a browser at desktop or phone width: "This site can't be reached - DNS_PROBE_FINISHED_NXDOMAIN".
- The public record for the domain shows it expired on 29 August 2026 and now carries "client hold" and "pending delete" at Squarespace Domains II LLC; ICANN's glossary says such a name drops from the registry after several days.
- Snapshots from 13 February 2026 show a small site - home, about, a services page, four service pages and contact - with a (973) 905-2129 call button and a "Get A Free Quote" form. None of it is reachable now.
- The WiserMap directory entry links to the same dead address.

**What it means for customers**

Anyone tapping "Website" on Google or WiserMap lands on a browser error and cannot see services, photos or a quote form. The profile and phone number still work, so calling is the practical route.

**How to fix it**

1. Log in at Squarespace Domains (the registrar on the public record) and ask whether the name can be renewed or restored - 0.5-1 h, owner.
2. If not, re-register it when it drops or pick a new .com; stop linking to the old address - 1-2 h plus waiting, owner.
3. Rebuild a small site (home, services, gallery, contact) with tap-to-call and a quote form - 4-8 h, web developer.
4. Change the website field on Google and WiserMap - 0.5-1.5 h, owner.
5. Turn on auto-renew and add a reminder or free uptime check - 0.5 h, owner.

**After the fix**

Add project photos and a description to the Google profile and invite recent customers to review, so the 5.0 rating has detail behind it.
