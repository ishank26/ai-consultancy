Website Health Check: Crystal Smiles LLC (Dr. Tejal Darji, DMD)

What we saw on 13 November 2026

  • crystalsmilesnj.com now redirects to a HugeDomains domain page; desktop and 390 px phone visits both stopped on Cloudflare's verification screen (screenshots saved); a plain fetch returned 403.
  • Wayback shows the practice's own site there from 2020 to June 2023; a July 2025 capture already redirects to HugeDomains.
  • RDAP: registered 27 Sep 2018, expires 27 Sep 2027, last changed 28 Sep 2026, registrar TurnCommerce/NameBright.
  • The Google profile (65 Rues Ln, (732) 257-4444, 4.8 stars) shows "Add website"; BizArchive still publishes www.crystalsmilesnj.com as the site.

What it means for customers

A patient who searches the practice name or follows an old directory link reaches a domain reseller's page, not the office. Services, insurance, hours and directions have to come from third-party listings or a phone call.

How to fix it

  1. Establish who controls the registrar account and whether the name can be recovered (1-2 hours).
  2. Remove the dead link from the Google profile and any directory still showing it (1-2 hours).
  3. Put a small site on a new or recovered domain: services, insurance, hours, new patients, tap-to-call (8-16 hours).
  4. Add an appointment request or booking link and connect it to the profile (2-4 hours).
  5. Turn on auto-renew, expiry alerts and uptime monitoring (about 1 hour).

After the fix

Online appointment requests let patients book outside office hours; the archived site's "Book an Appointment" buttons only opened a contact page.
