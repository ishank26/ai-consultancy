## What it means for customers

## How to fix it

1. Confirm whether the business ever held the domain, and who controls the registrar account (owner, 0.5–1 h).
2. If not, register a clean alternative with auto-renew instead of paying a resale price (owner/web person, 0.5–1 h).
3. Build a small site: galleries, service area, click-to-call, quote form (web person 8–16 h; owner 2–3 h).
4. Create and verify a Google Business Profile with the new site (owner, 1–2 h plus verification).
5. Add auto-renew, certificate and uptime alerts; keep listings on the new site (1–2 h).
