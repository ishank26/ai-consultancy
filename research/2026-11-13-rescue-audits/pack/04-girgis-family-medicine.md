2. Change the website link on both profiles the same day - owner or front desk; under 1 hour.

4. Reset passwords and add two-factor on registrar, DNS, hosting and Google - owner or developer; 1-2 hours.
5. Add link monitoring and re-check each profile monthly - developer; about 1 hour.

**After the fix**
