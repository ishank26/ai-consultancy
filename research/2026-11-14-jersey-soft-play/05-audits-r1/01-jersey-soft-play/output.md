# Opportunity Audit — Jersey Soft Play

Your packages are easy to read — $550–$750 for four hours — and reviews praise quick replies. On 14 November 2026 the site had no way to check availability or get a price; visitors reach you by phone, email, or a link out to a third-party form.

### 1. Let people book on your own site

*Now:* your "Check Availability" links land on /booking/, which has no form, then a 60-plus-field HoneyBook form behind a reCAPTCHA.

*Agent:* a short form there (date, town, venue, package) with an all-in estimate and delivery bands per zone, plus message answers on coverage and space.

*Impact:* a parent at 9pm sees price, hours and coverage and holds a date; the pilot counts enquiries before and after.

*Pilot (2–4 weeks):* the form, delivery bands, and a town page for East Brunswick (home town to confirm).

### 2. Quote each enquiry in minutes, not by hand

*Now:* your booking page says a date is held after availability, delivery, setup and price are confirmed; delivery is priced per enquiry, not published.

*Agent:* it reads each HoneyBook submission, prices the event, drafts the reply with a hold link, chases at 24 and 72 hours, then sends the setup checklist and a review request.

*Impact:* about 24–144 staff hours a year (10–20 enquiries a month x 15–40 minutes x 80–90% automated, our assumptions) at $31/hour (Counter and Rental Clerks, NJ, May 2025 mean, x1.25 benefits, our assumption) = about $744–$4,464 a year.

*Pilot (2–4 weeks):* draft-and-approve on live enquiries, a delivery-zone table, one measured response time.

### 3. One kit list behind every booking

*Now:* your packages page lists an "XL" bounce house at 7.5x7x6 ft for +$200; your enquiry form offers "XXL" at 10x10x11.5 ft for the same.

*Agent:* one kit list (item, colour, quantity, condition) behind each booking generates a packing list, load plan and phone run sheet.

*Impact:* 15–30 minutes of packing per event x 80–90% automated (our assumptions), about $6–$13 at $31/hour; and if 2–4 mis-specified events a year are avoided (our assumption), about $1,100–$3,000 at published prices.

*Pilot (2–4 weeks):* one list for 5–10 real bookings plus a photo condition log.

### Why this works

- The agent drafts; you approve. Discounts, weather and upstairs venues come to you.
- HoneyBook stays your back office; customer details stay in your own accounts.

### Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot on the booking page.
