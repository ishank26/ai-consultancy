# Opportunity Audit — Jersey Soft Play

Your packages are easy to read — $550–$750 for four hours, footprints spelled out — and reviews praise quick replies and clean setups. On 14 November 2026 we found no way to enquire on the site.

### 1. Let people book on your own site

*Now:* all five "Check Availability" links open /booking/, which has no form, then a HoneyBook tab asking 64 questions behind a reCAPTCHA.

*Agent:* a short form there (date, town, venue, package) with a plain all-in estimate and a published delivery band per zone, plus around-the-clock answers on coverage, space and surfaces by message. You approve anything non-standard.

*Impact:* a parent browsing at 9pm sees price, hours and coverage, and holds a date on your site; the pilot counts enquiries before and after.

*Pilot (2–4 weeks):* the form, delivery bands, a town page for East Brunswick.

### 2. Quote each enquiry in minutes, not by hand

*Now:* enquiries are answered by hand — availability, delivery, setup and price confirmed before a date is held — and delivery is priced per trip, never published.

*Agent:* it reads each HoneyBook submission, prices package, add-ons and delivery zone, drafts the reply with a hold link, chases at 24 and 72 hours, then sends the setup checklist and a review request. You approve every quote.

*Impact:* about 24–144 staff hours a year (10–20 enquiries a month x 15–40 minutes each x 80–90% automated; volume, time and share are our assumptions) at $31/hour (BLS OEWS mean for Counter and Rental Clerks, NJ, May 2025: $52,020 ÷ 2,080 = $25.01, republished by Factanker, x1.25 for benefits, our assumption) = about $744–$4,464 a year.

*Pilot (2–4 weeks):* draft-and-approve on live enquiries, a written delivery-zone table, one measured response time.

### 3. One kit list behind every booking

*Now:* the site's "XL" bounce house is 7.5x7x6 ft for +$200; the inquiry form's "XXL" is 10x10x11.5 ft for the same $200.

*Agent:* one kit list (item, colour, quantity, condition) that each booking draws from to generate a packing list, load plan and phone run sheet; you approve overrides and deposits.

*Impact (per event):* 1 event x 15–30 minutes prep x 80–90% automated (our assumption) = 12–27 minutes, about $6–$13 at $31/hour; 2–4 re-deliveries avoided a year at $550–$750 = about $1,100–$3,000.

*Pilot (2–4 weeks):* a single list for 5–10 real bookings plus a photo condition log.

### Why this works

- The agent drafts; you approve. Discounts, weather, upstairs venues and odd requests come to you.
- Nothing is replaced: HoneyBook stays the back office, your phone and email stay the channels, and customer details stay in your own accounts.

### Suggested first step

A 20-minute walkthrough, then a small paid pilot on the booking page.
